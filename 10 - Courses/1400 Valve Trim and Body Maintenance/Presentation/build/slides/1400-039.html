Inherent Flow Characteristic

  • Inherent flow characteristic
  • The relationship between the flow coefficient (Cv) and closure-member travel, from closed to rated travel, at a constant pressure drop across the valve

Provides max flow (Cv) at minimum travel

Provides a percentage of maximum control valve flow coefficient (Cv) directly proportional to valve travel

Equal increments of change in stem position will produce an equal percent change from the existing Cv.
