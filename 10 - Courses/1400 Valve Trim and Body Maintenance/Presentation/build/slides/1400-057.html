Basic Plug Maintenance

  • Minimum maintenance required for unbalanced plugs

Verify plug seat surface

Minor defects: lap plugs

Gouges: machine or replace

Plugs may be reused

Damage to surfaces prevent shutoff and increase damage from wear

Verify stem surface

Minor thread defects: polish with light sandpaper

Gouges: replace stem

Never reuse stem when replacing plug

Stem damage can damage packing
