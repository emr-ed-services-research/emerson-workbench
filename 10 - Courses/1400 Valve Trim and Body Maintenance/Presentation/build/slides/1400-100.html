Check Your Knowledge 2: Bench Set

You are bench-setting a 657 off the valve. At the lower bench-set pressure the stem first moves, but at the upper bench-set pressure the travel between your marks is less than the rated travel on the nameplate. What is the most likely cause?

  • A.  Packing friction is still acting on the actuator
  • B.  The wrong spring is installed, or its compression is set wrong
  • C.  The diaphragm is ruptured
  • D.  The loading-pressure gauge is reading high

B. The wrong spring is installed, or its compression is set wrong

100
