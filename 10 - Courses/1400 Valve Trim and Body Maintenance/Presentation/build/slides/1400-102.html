Re-Checking Travel on the Valve

  • Off the valve — friction-free
  • On the valve — with packing friction
  • 1Upper bench set (11 psig)
  • 2Bench set + friction ÷ diaphragm area

On the valve, full rated travel no longer happens at the bench-set pressure — it happens at bench set plus the extra pressure the packing friction adds.
