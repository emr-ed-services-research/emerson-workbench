Fail Action No Spring

  • No inherent (built-in) fail action
  • Instruments can be used to provide fail mode
  • Positioner - Fail open or closed on loss of command signal from control system
  • Trip Valve* - Fail open, closed, lock in last on loss of supply pressure
  • Solenoid* - Fail open or closed on command from control system
  • *Volume tank may be required
