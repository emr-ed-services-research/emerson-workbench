Rotary Actuator Mounting Styles

  • Right-hand / Left-hand mounting
  • Look in direction of flow
  • What side of line is actuator mounted
  • Style A, B, C, D
  • Determines lever rotation (CW/CCW) with increasing air to top of actuator
  • Need properly matched to operation of rotary valve (i.e. HPBV should rotate CCW to open)
  • Position 1, 2, 3, 4
  • Determine required orientation of actuator to line
  • Any orientation should produce correct rotation of the lever
  • Additional support may be required for position 2 and 4 with larger actuators
