Zero and Span Calibration

  • Positioner calibrated to saturate at travel extremes
  • Saturation = point that output is 0 psi or full supply
  • Ensures valve will fully open for full flow capacity and close for rated shutoff
  • Set during Zero and Span calibration
  • When calibrated correctly saturation should occur just inside limits of control signal span
  • Typical saturation points for direct acting positioner:
  • 3-3.5 psi for zero and 14.5 – 15 psi for span
  • 6-7 psi for zero and 29 – 30 for span
  • 4-4.5 mA for zero and 19.5 to 20 mA for span
