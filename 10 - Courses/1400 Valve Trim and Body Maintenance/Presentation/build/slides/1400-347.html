Exercise 1 – Connect to Device

  • Follow the steps below to connect the power and the Emerson Communicator AMS Trex to the FIELDVUE digital valve controller:
  • Connect current source (required for ValveLink Mobile) to the Loop Power terminals in the terminal box
  • Note: Polarity from the current source to the device must match
  • Connect leads from the HART communication terminals on the communicator to the Comm Terminals in the terminal box
  • Note: Connecting the leads for HART communication is not polarity sensitive
