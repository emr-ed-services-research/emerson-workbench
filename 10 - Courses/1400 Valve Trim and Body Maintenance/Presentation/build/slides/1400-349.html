HART® or FOUNDATION™ fieldbus

  • ValveLink Mobile supports both HART and FOUNDATION fieldbus devices
  • Ensure that the communication leads for the communicator are in the proper terminals located on the top of the communicator
  • HART communication is not polarity sensitive from the communicator to the device
  • Fieldbus communication requires that polarity from the communicator terminals match positive and negative on the device
  • Select the communication protocol of the device you are connected to
