Main Menu Connected – Instrument Mode

  • Selecting “Done” from connection menu returns to Main Menu
  • Instrument mode is now indicated in bottom right of screen
  • Mode types are:
  • In Service – device will respond to signal change from controller – must be in this mode during normal operation
  • Out of Service – device will not respond to signal change from controller – typically put in this mode by the user during configuration, calibration, and diagnostic routines.
