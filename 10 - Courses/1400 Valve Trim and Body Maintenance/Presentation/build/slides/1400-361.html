Actuator Information – Model and Size

  • If a manufacturer is selected from the list a list of models is shown
  • Selecting model will identify type of actuator (Spring and diaphragm, Piston, Sliding Stem, Rotary)
  • Populates information into device Spec Sheet
  • A list of actuator sizes is available after selecting model
  • Selecting size identifies
  • Diaphragm or piston surface area
  • Used to determine correct factory tuning parameter
  • Populates information into device Spec Sheet
