Auto Travel Calibration – Finding End Points

  • Finding high drive end point
  • Full 100% drive current applied to I/P – full output pressure sent through output A
  • Valve strokes until contact with mechanical stop
  • Travel sensor reading establishes first travel end point
  • Finding low drive end point
  • 0% drive current applied to I/P – 0 psi output pressure sent through output A
  • Valve strokes until contact with mechanical stop
  • Travel sensor reading establishes second travel end point
  • Travel end point are related as 0 and 100% travel of actuator
  • 0% travel equals sensor reading for valve closed
  • 100% travel equals sensor reading for valve open
  • Requires correct information to be entered during Setup Wizard or in Detailed Setup
