Exercise 2 – Auto Travel Calibration

  • Follow the steps below to calibrate the travel for the FIELDVUE digital valve controller and valve assembly.
  • Following the Setup Wizard select to continue with Auto Travel Calibration, or from the ValveLink Mobile main menu select Calibration then select Auto Travel Calibration.
  • Ensure at least 12mA applied to the device to ensure it is properly powered
  • Relay Adjustment (Piston Only) – This procedure will require adjustment of the relay prior to running the auto travel calibration and will be discussed later in this chapter. Proceed with auto travel calibration without doing procedure.
  • DVC should move valve to automatically find high and low drive stop references
  • Select “Yes” to move the valve and find pressure range high and low
  • With auto travel calibration complete select done
  • Put device “In Service”
  • Use current source to move valve, and indicate corresponding travel positions:
