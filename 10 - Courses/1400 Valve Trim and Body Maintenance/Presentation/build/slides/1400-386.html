Relay Adjustment – Relay A

  • Properly configures Relay A for operation of single acting or double acting assemblies
  • Relay adjustment typically ran during auto travel calibration for double acting assembly
  • Can also be ran as independent calibration through calibration menu
  • No adjustments for Relay B or C
  • Adjustments
  • Double Acting
  • Perform relay adjust procedure during calibration
  • Balances relay for dual outputs (A and B)
  • Single Acting
  • Adjust star wheel completely up in the - direction prior to calibration
  • Isolates output B, DVC only uses output A
