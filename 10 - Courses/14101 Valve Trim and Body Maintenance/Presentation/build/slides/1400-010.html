Safety in Training

  • Training is not like the production area and requires attention.
  • There maybe increased safety risks due to
  • Inexperienced learners
  • New or different tools and work processes
  • Safety IS training
  • Use PPE: safety glasses, safety shoes, gloves
  • Follow safety warnings
  • Pay attention when working on demo equipment
  • “Train as you work” also means learning to do the job safely in training to apply it back on the job
  • Don’t become complacent during practice!
