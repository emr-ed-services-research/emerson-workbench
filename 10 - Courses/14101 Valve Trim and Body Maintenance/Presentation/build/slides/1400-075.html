Jam Style Packing Compression

  • Use anti-seize
  • Lubricate Packing Studs
  • Lubricate Packing Nuts
  • Torque as required

GRAPHITE - Tighten the nuts until the maximum recommended torque is reached. Then, loosen the packing flange nuts and retighten to the recommended minimum torque.
