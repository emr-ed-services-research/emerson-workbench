Bench Set — Off the Valve

Raise the loading pressure to the lower bench-set value and mark the stem at first movement; raise to the upper value and mark again. Off the valve, no packing friction — the span between the two marks must equal the nameplate rated travel. Direct-acting (657) shown; a 667 stem retracts, same procedure.
