9. Fisher™ Rotary Actuator Maintenance

After completing this module the student will be able to:

  1. Identify Fisher™ Spring and Diaphragm Rotary Actuators
  2. Identify Fisher™ Piston Rotary Actuators
  3. Describe common maintenance procedures for Spring and Diaphragm and Piston Actuators
  4. Identify Right-Hand and Left-Hand Mounting
  5. Define push down to open versus push down to close
  6. Select a valve and actuator orientation to produce a desired fail state
  7. Change actuator and valve orientation
