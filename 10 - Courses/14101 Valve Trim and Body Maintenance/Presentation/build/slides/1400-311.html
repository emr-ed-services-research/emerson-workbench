Principle of Operation

Control signal received in Terminal box and sent to Printed Wiring Board (PWB)

PWB compares control signal to feedback from travel sensor – calculates error

PWB adjusts current signal that is sent to I/P reported as Drive Signal

I/P moves flapper in relation to nozzle to create modulated output pressure to relay

Modulated pressure applied to relay beam to open and close supply valves

Relay outputs supply air to actuator

Travel position measured by hall effect sensor

Travel position sent back to PWB

Process repeats until error eliminated equilibrium found
