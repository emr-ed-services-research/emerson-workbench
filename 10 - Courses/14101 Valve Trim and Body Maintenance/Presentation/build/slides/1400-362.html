Actuator Information – Volume Booster

  • Volume Booster
  • used to increase volume of air for large assemblies
  • Often used for fast stroking speeds
  • Device output applied as input to volume booster
  • Reduced surface area compared to actuator diaphragm or piston
  • Special device factory tuning set applied for improved control
  • Volume booster bypass setting requires calibration independent of DVC calibration
