Auto Travel Calibration – Adjusting Output Bias

  • Valve assembly stroked to approximately 50% travel
  • Program performs series of bump tests
  • PWB monitoring relay position
  • Checks for valve response to drive signal changes
  • Verifies assembly maintains stability
  • Helps confirm tuning of assembly
  • Collects/stores information for use with performance diagnostics
  • Large valves
  • May not be enough time for test to complete
  • Auto Travel Calibration will abort
  • Try Manual Travel Calibration
