15. Workshop 2: Rotary

After completing this module the student will be able to:

  1. Repair and maintain a Fisher rotary spring and diaphragm actuators (1052 and 2052 actuators)
  2. Repair and maintain a Fisher 1061 piston actuator
  3. Repair and maintain Fisher high performance butterfly valves
  4. Repair and maintain Fisher eccentric plug valves
  5. Repair and maintain Fisher Vee-Ball valves
