Check Your Knowledge: Rotary Valves & Flow Characteristics

You're diagnosing a rotary control valve with a quick-opening cage. Two symptoms: (1) the controller output has to swing several percent before the valve moves at all, and (2) once it does move, a small signal change right near the closed position produces a big jump in flow. What's responsible for each symptom?

  • A.  Friction/backlash (deadband) causes (1); the quick-opening cage's own shape causes (2)
  • B.  The V-notch ball's rangeability causes (1); the rotary actuator's lever ratio causes (2)
  • C.  The positioner's bench set causes (1); an equal-percentage cage would cause (2)
  • D.  The eccentric disk's offset shaft causes (1); a linear cage causes (2)
