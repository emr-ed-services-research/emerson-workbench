Bonnet Types

  1. 1Typical bonnet — bolts to the body with stud bolts; houses the packing box and mounts the actuator
  2. 2Bonnet variations — extend the basic bolted shape for extra clearance or insulation
  3. 3Fabricated extension bonnet — lengthens the packing box away from the process fluid, for cryogenic or very hot service
