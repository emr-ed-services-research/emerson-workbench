How an Analog I/P Positioner Works

  1. 1A 4-20 mA current signal drives the converter, which produces a proportional pneumatic signal at the bellows
  2. 2From there, the same beam / feedback-axis / nozzle-flapper / relay loop as the pneumatic positioner sets the output pressure
  3. 3The assembled unit — converter housing, gauges, mounting bracket — bolted onto the actuator in place of a pneumatic positioner
