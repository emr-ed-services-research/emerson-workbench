How an I/P Transducer Works

  1. 1The input current signal positions the deflector bar between the two nozzles, changing pilot pressure
  2. 2Pilot pressure drives the booster stage and sets the pneumatic output directly — with no position feedback at all
  3. 3The assembled transducer, mounted on the valve in place of a positioner
