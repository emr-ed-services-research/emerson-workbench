Amplifying Flow With a Volume Booster

A volume booster amplifies the flow delivered to the actuator without changing the signal's own pressure — add one wherever a large or fast-stroking actuator would otherwise outrun the positioner's own limited output capacity.

After Control Valve Handbook, 6th ed., Figs. 4.7, 4.8 — used directly, combined side by side. Component Index: cvh-cmp-4.7, cvh-cmp-4.8.
