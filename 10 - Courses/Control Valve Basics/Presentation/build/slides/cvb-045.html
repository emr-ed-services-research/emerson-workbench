Check Your Knowledge: Positioners, Transducers & Boosters

A large, fast-stroking rotary actuator isn’t reaching full stroke quickly enough during process upsets, even though its positioner is working correctly and commanding the full pressure swing every time. Which accessory addresses this, and why?

  • A.  A digital valve controller — its microprocessor reacts faster than a mechanical positioner’s beam and flapper
  • B.  An I/P transducer — removing position feedback lets the signal reach the actuator with no delay
  • C.  A volume booster — amplifies the pneumatic flow to the actuator without changing the signal’s own pressure
  • D.  A second positioner — closes a tighter local loop than a single instrument can

C · Volume Booster
