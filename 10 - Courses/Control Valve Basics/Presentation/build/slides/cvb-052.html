During a shutdown-valve walk-down you find: the trip valve is already sitting in its tripped condition, the SOV driving the actuator carries a 4-port schematic symbol on the P&ID, and the P&ID calls the arrangement out as 1oo2 with a second SOV elsewhere in the loop. What does this tell you about the actuator and how the trip could have happened?

  • A.  The actuator is double-acting, and either SOV alone could have caused this trip
  • B.  The actuator is single-acting (spring-return), and both SOVs had to agree before this trip could happen
  • C.  The actuator is double-acting, and both SOVs had to agree before this trip could happen
  • D.  The 4-port symbol only describes the SOV's own pilot stage, and says nothing about the actuator

A · Double-acting, either SOV could trip it
