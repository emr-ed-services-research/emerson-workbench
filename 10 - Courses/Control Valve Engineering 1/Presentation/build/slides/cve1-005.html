The Feedback Control Loop

  • 1Process — the variable actually being controlled (pressure, flow, level, temperature…)
  • 2Sensor & Transmitter — measure the process variable, carry it onward as the Controlled Variable
  • 3Controller — compares the measurement to setpoint, computes the correction
  • 4Control Valve — the final control element; carries the correction into the process as the Manipulated Variable — the whole assembly (body, trim, actuator, accessories), not the body alone

After Control Valve Handbook, 6th ed., Fig. 1.1 — used directly, block labels retained. Component Index: cvh-cmp-feedback-control-loop.
