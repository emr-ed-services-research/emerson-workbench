Sliding-Stem Valves — Globe, Angle, Three-Way

  • 1Globe body — the baseline: straight-through ports under a spring-and-diaphragm actuator
  • 2Assembly stack-up — actuator, then bonnet, then body; trim (plug, cage, seat ring) sits inside the body, under the bonnet
  • 3Angle body — inlet and outlet perpendicular instead of straight-through; same sliding-stem action
  • 4Three-way body — one body, three connections; combines or diverts flow instead of simple two-port throttling

After Control Valve Handbook, 6th ed., Figs. 1.2, 1.3, 1.4, 1.8 — used directly; Fig. 1.3's own printed 1–11 label its parts, a separate scheme from this list's 1–4.
