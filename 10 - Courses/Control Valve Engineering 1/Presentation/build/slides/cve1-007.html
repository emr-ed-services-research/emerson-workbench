Four Forces, Not One

  • 1Total actuator force = A (unbalance) + B (seat load) + C (packing friction) + D (other) — stroking thrust covers only part of A
  • 2A comes from fluid pressure at shutoff: net pressure differential × net unbalance area. This valve is single-seated and unbalanced, so its full port area counts — a balanced trim would route pressure to both sides of the plug to cancel most, but never all, of that force
  • 3Port dia. 2-5/16 in → unbalance area 4.20 in² (Fig. 5.2). A = 4.20 × 50 psi = 210 lbf
  • 4Fig. 5.3 at Class II, 50 psi ≈ 15 lbf/in. Circumference = π × 2.3125 = 7.27 in, so B ≈ 15 × 7.27 = 109 lbf
