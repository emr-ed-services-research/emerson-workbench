Inherent vs. Installed Characteristic and Gain

  • 1Bench (inherent) — flow-vs-travel and valve gain measured at a fixed, held ΔP
  • 2Installed (real loop) — the same relationship once the process lets ΔP drift as flow, upstream pressure, and downstream demand change
  • Valve gain — the local slope of the characteristic curve: how much flow changes per increment of travel
  • Loop stability needs roughly constant installed gain across the operating range — a spec built only from the inherent curve never checks this
