Rangeability — the Cv-Max-to-Cv-Min Ratio

  • 1Cv-min — smallest flow coefficient the valve still controls accurately, within the stated deviation limit
  • 2Cv-max — largest flow coefficient the valve still controls accurately, same limit
  • 3Rangeability = Cv-max ÷ Cv-min (or max-to-min controllable flow) — a rated property, looked up on the data sheet
  • Handbook reference case: control out to 100× the minimum controllable flow → rangeability of 100:1
