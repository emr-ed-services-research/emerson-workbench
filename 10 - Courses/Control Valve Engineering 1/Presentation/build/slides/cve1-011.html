The Real Total, Worked

  • 1Port dia. 1-7/8 in → unbalance area 2.76 in² (Fig. 5.2). A = 2.76 × 60 psi ≈ 166 lbf
  • 2Fig. 5.3 at Class III, 60 psi ≈ 45 lbf/in. Circumference = π × 1.875 = 5.89 in, so B ≈ 45 × 5.89 ≈ 265 lbf
  • 3C ≈ 25 lbf, D ≈ 10 lbf. Total required = 166 + 265 + 25 + 10 ≈ 466 lbf — nearly double the 250 lbf actually rated. B alone (265 lbf) exceeds the whole actuator's rated output
