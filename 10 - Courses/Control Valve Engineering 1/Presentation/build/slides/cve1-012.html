Comparing Rated Rangeability to Required Turndown

  1. State the loop's required turndown — the largest flow the process will demand ÷ the smallest flow it must still be controlled at
  2. Pull the candidate valve's rated rangeability off its data sheet (Cv-max ÷ Cv-min at the stated deviation limit)
  3. Compare the two directly — the valve's rated rangeability must meet or exceed the loop's required turndown
