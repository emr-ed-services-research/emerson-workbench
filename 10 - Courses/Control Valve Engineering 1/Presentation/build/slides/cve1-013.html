!Confirm Rated Rangeability Clears the Loop's Required Turndown

A valve whose rated rangeability doesn't clear the loop's required turndown cannot resolve flow changes once demand drops below the bottom of its usable range — the loop hunts, cycles, or loses control at low flow, and no amount of controller retuning fixes a valve that is mechanically incapable of resolving that flow. This is a specification failure, not an operating one: it passes every static check — correct Cv at design flow, correct pressure class — and still fails the loop the moment demand turns down.
