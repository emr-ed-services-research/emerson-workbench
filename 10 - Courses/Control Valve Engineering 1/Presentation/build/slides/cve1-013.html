Flashing vs. Cavitation: What Actually Erodes Metal

  • 1Pressure at the vena contracta drops to or below vapor pressure → liquid flashes to vapor bubbles
  • 2If downstream pressure recovers above vapor pressure, those bubbles collapse violently
  • 3That collapse — not the flashing itself — is what erodes metal. No recovery above vapor pressure means no collapse damage.
