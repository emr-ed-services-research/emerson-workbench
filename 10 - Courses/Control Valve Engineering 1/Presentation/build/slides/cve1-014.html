High Recovery Means Higher Cavitation Risk

  • 1A high-recovery design (e.g. a streamlined ball valve) recovers pressure faster and higher after the vena contracta than a low-recovery design
  • 2Which is exactly why it's MORE prone to cavitation for the same conditions, not less
  • 3More recovery means a higher chance of crossing back above vapor pressure
