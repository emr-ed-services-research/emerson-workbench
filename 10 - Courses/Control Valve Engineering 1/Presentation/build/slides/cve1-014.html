Deadband on the Open Loop

  • 1The open loop is deadband made visible — reverse direction anywhere on the plot, and the process variable doesn't move again until the whole gap is crossed
  • 2Backlash — slack or looseness in a mechanical connection — can produce it
  • 3More often: static friction (stiction) — the force that must be overcome before there is any relative motion at all between two surfaces
  • A spec that never asks how much stiction a packing/trim combination carries is ignoring the mechanism most likely to produce this exact gap
