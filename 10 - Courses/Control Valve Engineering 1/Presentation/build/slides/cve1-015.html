Deadband's Two Mechanical Causes

TermWhat it isCauses deadband?
BacklashSlack or looseness in a mechanical connection — a linkage, a coupling, a loose stem connectorYes
Static friction (stiction)The force that must be overcome before there is any relative motion at all between two surfaces — most often at the packing or seating surfacesYes — the more common of the two
Dynamic (running) frictionThe smaller force that sustains motion once stiction is already overcomeNo — but a packing spec'd only against running friction can still stiction-deadband

Originated comparison table — no component is registered for either cause (Control Valve Handbook 6th ed. §1.4/§1.5, pp. 28-29, prose only; cvh-cmp-deadband-graph anchors the deadband plot elsewhere in this module, and does not distinguish backlash from stiction).
