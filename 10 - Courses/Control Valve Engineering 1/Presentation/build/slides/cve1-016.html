Check Your Knowledge: Cavitation and Flashing

A streamlined, high-recovery ball valve and a lower-recovery globe valve see identical upstream and downstream pressures. Which is more prone to cavitation, and why?

  • A.  The globe valve — more internal turbulence causes more damage directly
  • B.  The ball valve — its faster, higher pressure recovery is more likely to cross back above vapor pressure
  • C.  Neither — cavitation risk depends only on upstream/downstream pressure, not recovery
  • D.  The globe valve — lower recovery means the vena contracta pressure never drops as low

B · More recovery means a higher chance of crossing back above vapor pressure — the counter-intuitive result the source itself states directly.
