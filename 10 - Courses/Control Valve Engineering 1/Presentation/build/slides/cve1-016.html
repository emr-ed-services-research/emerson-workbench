Dynamic Response — Dead Time, Time Constant, T63

  • 1Dashed — the step change applied to the signal at t = 0; solid — the valve assembly's actual output
  • 2Td (dead time) — the interval before any response is detectable at all
  • 3Time constant — measured from the end of Td to the point marked T63, where output reaches 63% of its final value
  • T63 = Td + time constant — what "response time" usually means for the whole assembly, not the trim alone
