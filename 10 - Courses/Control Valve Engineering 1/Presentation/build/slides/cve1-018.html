Check Your Knowledge — Gain, Rangeability, Deadband & Response

A valve was spec'd with the correct Cv at design flow and the correct pressure class. In service it controls smoothly at high flow, but hunts and loses control whenever demand drops to a small fraction of design flow — even though bench data confirm the actuator's dead time and time constant are both fast. What is the most likely cause?

  • A.  The valve's rated rangeability doesn't clear the loop's actual required turndown
  • B.  Backlash or stiction in the assembly is producing deadband
  • C.  Installed gain has drifted from the bench-tested (inherent) curve as ΔP shifts with flow
  • D.  The assembly is behaving as a second-order system and overshooting

A · Rated rangeability doesn't clear required turndown
