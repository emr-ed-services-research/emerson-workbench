Process Variability — the Cost of Spread

  1. 1Upper curve — wide spread: a large 2σ band forces the set point back from the quality limit as a safety margin, wasting product/energy on conservative operation
  2. 2Lower curve — narrow spread: tight control lets the same set point sit closer to the limit with no off-spec product
  3. 3Up to 80% of real control loops fail to adequately reduce this spread — the control valve assembly (valve, actuator, and positioner as one unit, not sized separately) is a frequent, overlooked cause
