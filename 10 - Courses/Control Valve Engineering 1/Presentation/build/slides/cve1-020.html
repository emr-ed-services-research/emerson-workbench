The Economic Case for a Better Valve

Every real valve design falls somewhere between the "Manual" line (no control at all) and "Minimum Variability" (the calculated ideal) — valves that meet the same static purchase spec do not perform the same dynamically. Replacing Valve B with Valve A at a 2.0-second closed-loop time constant measures a 1.4% variability improvement — a real, quantified cost, not just "this one seems better."
