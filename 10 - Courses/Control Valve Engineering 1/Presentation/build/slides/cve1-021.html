Oversizing Distorts Installed Gain

  1. 1Oversizing puts too much of the loop's total gain into the valve itself — crowding out the controller's own share (best performance comes from the controller holding most of the gain, not the valve)
  2. 2Oversizing forces the valve to operate more of its life at low openings, where seal friction is worst — exaggerating the resulting friction/deadband penalty on variability
  3. 3Net effect, whatever the valve's inherent characteristic: quick-opening behavior (high, hard-to-control gain) at low lift, nearly flat (uncontrollable) at high travel — the usable control range narrows to a fraction of total travel
