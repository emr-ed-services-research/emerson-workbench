Globe Bodies — Single- vs. Double-Ported

  • 1Single-ported (post-guided) — the default for NPS 1-4 process service; full unbalanced port area unless cage/retainer trim balances it
  • 2Double-ported (reverse-acting) — balances dynamic plug force across two ports for a smaller actuator; shutoff ceiling around Class II/III
