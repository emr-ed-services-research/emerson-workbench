Rotary Bodies — Butterfly, Segmented Ball, Full-Port Ball

  • 1Butterfly — disc rotates on a shaft; shaft-to-disc-center offset sets seal wear and rangeability
  • 2Segmented/V-notch ball — a contoured segment shears rather than clogs on slurries and fibrous fluids
  • 3Full-port ball — minimal flow restriction, allows pigging; needs 15–20° of rotation before it starts controlling
