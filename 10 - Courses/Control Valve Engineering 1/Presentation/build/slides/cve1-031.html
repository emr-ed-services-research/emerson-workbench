The Eight Body Families

Globe — flow turns through a seat-and-plug port; the default sliding-stem throttling body.See slide 24. Component Index: cvh-cmp-single-ported-globe-valve-body.
Angle — a single-port globe variant that also serves as a pipe elbow.See slide 25. Component Index: cvh-cmp-flanged-angle-valve-body.
Three-way — converges or diverges flow through a third connection, throttled mid-travel.See slide 25. Component Index: cvh-cmp-three-way-globe-valve-cutaway.
Rotary (butterfly/ball) — a shaft rotates a disc, segment, or ball across the bore instead of sliding a stem.See slide 26. Component Index: cvh-cmp-butterfly-shaft-offset-disc-center.
Gate — a wedge slides across a straight-through body; on/off only, never a throttling spec.See slide 27. Component Index: cvh-cmp-solid-wedge-gate-valve.
Diaphragm — a flexible diaphragm closes against a weir; no metal trim in the flow path.See slide 28. Component Index: cvh-cmp-weir-type-diaphragm-valve.
Pinch — a flexible sleeve collapses shut; the choice for slurry and solids service.See slide 29. Component Index: cvh-cmp-air-operated-pinch-valve.
Plug — a tapered or eccentric plug rotates in the body; eccentric variants throttle, lubricated variants are on/off only.See slide 30. Component Index: cvh-cmp-eccentric-plug-valve-body, cvh-cmp-lubricated-plug-valve.
