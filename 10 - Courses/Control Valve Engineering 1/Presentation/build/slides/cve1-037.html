Specifying for a Low-Cv Service

When a body is specified oversized against today's actual flow — deliberately, for future capacity, or by a sizing error — restricted-capacity trim (commonly standardized around 40% of full-size capacity) is the standard spec call: stable control today, with no pipeline reducer or custom-sized body, and full-size parts can be swapped back in later once flow actually grows.
