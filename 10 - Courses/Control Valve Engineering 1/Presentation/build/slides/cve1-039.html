Before You Choose a Rotary Subtype

Every rotary vendor calls their own line “universal” — segmented ball, full-port ball, eccentric plug, and butterfly are all marketed as if any one of them fits any rotary duty. None of them do: each subtype’s own construction sets a hard ceiling on rangeability, shutoff class, or erosion resistance that no actuator or trim engineering downstream can fix later. Specifying by which name sounds toughest — not by which construction the service conditions actually require — is a real spec-liability risk.
