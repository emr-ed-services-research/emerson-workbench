Check-Valve Styles — Swing, Lever-Weight, Tilting

  • 1Swing check — the baseline hinged-disk design; less flow resistance fully open than a lift check, but can slam and chatter on shock closure
  • 2Lever-and-weight swing check — forces immediate closure on flow reversal to minimize the plain swing check's own shock/chatter problem
  • 3Tilting-disk swing check — flow passes both above and below the disk for greater stability, most advantageous above 6 in. and on higher-pressure, low-flow-rate service
