Bolted-Flange vs. Welded End Connections

Bolted-Flange
Flat-face, raised-face, or ring-type-joint — easily removed for maintenance and available across every valve size, near absolute zero to about 815°C (1500°F). Ring-type-joint reaches the highest pressure of the three, to 15,000 psig, but is not suited to high temperature.
Welded
Socket-weld (to about NPS 2) or butt-weld (NPS 2-1/2 and larger) — leak-tight at all pressures and temperatures and economical to install, but far harder to remove from the line once in service. A welded-ends spec narrows the valve choice to sliding-stem construction only.
