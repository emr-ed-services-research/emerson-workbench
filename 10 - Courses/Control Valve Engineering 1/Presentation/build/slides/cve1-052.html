Verify the Rated Pressure Class

  1. Identify the bonnet construction — pressure-seal already floors the class at 900; a bolted bonnet leaves it open anywhere from 150 to 1500.
  2. Confirm the body material — cast iron (tops out at Class 800) or steel (covers 150 through 2500) — each has its own dimension-table family.
  3. Read the nominal pipe size and measure the actual face-to-face dimension on the part.
  4. Match the measured face-to-face value against the ANSI table for the confirmed material — the class whose table value matches is the verified rated class, not whatever a worn or missing nameplate shows.
