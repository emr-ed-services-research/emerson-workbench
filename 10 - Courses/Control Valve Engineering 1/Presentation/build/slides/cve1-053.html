Body-Family Selection — Pressure Drop, Capacity & Size

Size bandRecommended body styleRationale
Below 3 in.General-purpose sliding-stemBest performance and service life for a minimal price premium
4–6 in.Transitional (eccentric plug or ball)Favored on body-material cost and capacity
8 in. and largerHigh-performance butterflyEconomical on shutoff and capacity well beyond a globe design
Any size, high ΔP or shutoffSliding-stemRugged moving parts win where available pressure drop is large
Any size, small available ΔPRotaryMuch higher maximum capacity for a given body size, where capacity is the constraint, not pressure drop

Past the largest sizes, the remaining choices become a matter of price versus other operational advantages — not a hard technical criterion.
