A vertical reheat-steam line needs a valve that both isolates the line AND stops steam from returning to the turbine on a trip. The body-bonnet detail on the part in front of you shows a bolted bonnet with no nameplate; the piping spec calls for Class 600. What do you specify, and how do you confirm the class?

  • A.  A plain stop valve, sized from the line's nominal pipe size alone
  • B.  A stop-check (or dedicated check) valve; confirm the class by measuring face-to-face against the steel ANSI table for the body material, not by the missing nameplate
  • C.  Any check valve is fine here; the bolted bonnet alone already confirms Class 900 or higher, so no further check is needed
  • D.  A stop-check valve; since the nameplate is missing, assume the lowest class the body could be built to and proceed
