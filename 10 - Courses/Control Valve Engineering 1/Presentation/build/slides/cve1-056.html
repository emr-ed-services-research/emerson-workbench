Linear vs. Equal-Percentage

Linear
Flow coefficient directly proportional to travel — constant gain at constant ΔP. Fits a loop where the valve carries nearly all the system's pressure drop (liquid-level control, constant-gain flow loops).
Equal-Percentage
Gain rises with flow — the same percent change in travel produces a small change at low flow, a large one at high flow. Fits the more common case where piping and process equipment absorb most of the drop.
