Inherent vs. Installed — the Open Question

  • 1Inherent — Cv vs. travel at a fixed, held ΔP (ch1-m2's baseline)
  • 2Installed — the same relationship once the valve sits in a real loop, where its own share of the system's total pressure drop is free to vary as flow, upstream pressure, and downstream demand change
  • Installed gain — the local slope of the installed curve — is what actually governs loop stability, not the catalog's bench-tested curve
  • The open question this module answers: how far the two diverge, and what controls it — the next two slides turn "they diverge" into a predictable, checkable relationship
