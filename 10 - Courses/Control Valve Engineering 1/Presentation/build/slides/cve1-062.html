Pressure-Drop Ratio Drives the Divergence

  • 1High ratio — the valve holds most of the system ΔP — installed tracks close to the inherent curve
  • 2Low ratio — piping/process equipment hold most of the ΔP, and vary it as load shifts — installed gain distorts heavily, regardless of which inherent curve was ordered
  • Equal-percentage is paired deliberately with a process vessel's own falling gain — its rising-with-flow gain nets out to a roughly linear, well-behaved installed characteristic
  • Target: loop gain (every device's gain except the controller) held within about a 4:1 spread across the operating range — the EnTech consensus figure, a practitioner benchmark, not a hard physical law
