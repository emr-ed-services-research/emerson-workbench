Check Your Knowledge — Installed Characteristic & Rangeability

A sliding-stem globe valve will hold about 20% of the total system pressure drop at design flow — the rest is absorbed by the process vessel and piping. The loop needs 20:1 turndown, and the candidate trim is rated for 30:1 rangeability with no positioner planned. Which characteristic should you specify, and does the candidate clear the turndown requirement?

  • A.  Equal-percentage; yes — 30:1 rated rangeability clears the 20:1 requirement
  • B.  Linear; yes — 30:1 rated rangeability clears the 20:1 requirement
  • C.  Equal-percentage; no — a characterized positioner is required first to reach 20:1 turndown
  • D.  Linear; no — the valve must be replaced with a rotary body to reach 20:1 turndown

A · Equal-percentage, clears (30:1 > 20:1)
