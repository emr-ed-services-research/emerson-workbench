Specifying the Construction to Meet the Class

Duty situationConfirm this firstConstruction to verify
Occasional-cycling isolation duty, some seat leakage tolerableRead the required class off the table just taught — don't default upwardSeat material & seat load sized to the confirmed class, not over-built
Toxic or high-hazard block valve, near-zero visible leakage requiredConfirm Class V or VI is genuinely justified by the duty, not assumedSeat material, seat preparation, and seat load all matched together — the class number alone is a factory-test result, not a field guarantee
Any of the above, at high pressure drop (ΔP)Same confirmed class applies — ΔP doesn't change which class the duty needsAn under-built seat here risks trim erosion and eventual trim destruction, not just a leakage miss

A leakage class on a spec sheet is a factory-test comparison basis, not a field-leakage guarantee — meeting it in service depends on seat material, preparation, and load together, and skipping that check costs the most at high ΔP.
