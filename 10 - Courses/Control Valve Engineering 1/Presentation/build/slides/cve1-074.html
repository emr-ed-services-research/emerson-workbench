ANSI B16.34's Four Pressure-Temperature Rating Tiers

TierReached byConstruction requirement
Standard ClassNormal ANSI 150–4500 ratingsPublished for a variety of materials — most products use this tier
Intermediate StandardExtra body/bonnet wall thickness & stronger body-to-bonnet bolting — no NDE requiredButt-weld-end (BWE) valves only
Special ClassUltrasonic or radiographic testing (NDE) of the body & bonnet castingsAny BWE globe or angle valve
Intermediate SpecialBoth Special Class's NDE and Intermediate Standard's extra wall thickness/boltingBWE valves that already carry intermediate ratings
NDE method (radiographic, ultrasonic, magnetic-particle, or liquid-penetrant — B16.34's own methods preferred over the comparable MSS standards for broader acceptance) gates every tier above Intermediate Standard, and the personnel performing it must be SNT-TC-1A qualified — rating class and personnel qualification are coupled requirements, not independent purchasing choices. All three non-standard tiers carry real pricing implications.
