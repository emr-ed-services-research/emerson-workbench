Verifying a Stated Body/Bonnet Class

Nameplate claimsCheck this before accepting itWhat's required to sign off
Standard Class (e.g. Class 300)The class's maximum allowable pressure falls as temperature rises — re-check the nameplate class against the real service pressure AND temperature, not the class's headline ratingAdequate once the class covers the actual service condition at that temperature; no further documentation needed
Intermediate Standard, Special, or Intermediate Special claimedConfirm the valve is genuinely butt-weld-end (BWE) construction — no non-standard tier is valid on any other end connectionExtra wall-thickness/bolting records (intermediate tiers) and/or NDE records — radiographic, ultrasonic, magnetic-particle, or liquid-penetrant, by SNT-TC-1A-qualified personnel (special tiers) — on file; without them, the class hasn't actually been earned
Any class, any tierConfirm soft parts (elastomers, PTFE) stay within their real temperature limits, and trim material matches the service's corrosion/erosion/flashing/cavitation profileTrim carried over from body material by default is not sufficient on its own — suitability has to be confirmed at the actual service condition

A stated class is a claim, not a fact, until the service condition, the construction, and the documentation all check out together.
