Check Your Knowledge — P-T Rating & Code Jurisdiction

A control valve carries a Class 300 nameplate and is installed on ordinary power-plant piping — not connected to the boiler drum, and not sitting on the boiler-external piping segment either. The service pressure and temperature both check out fine against Class 300 at that temperature. The vendor's quote also claims an “Intermediate Special” rating with no NDE or wall-thickness/bolting documentation on file, and cites MSS SP-66 as the applicable design standard. Which of these is actually worth challenging before you accept the quote?

  • A.  The Class 300 nameplate itself, because a pressure class isn't valid on ordinary power-plant piping — only inside boiler-external piping
  • B.  The Intermediate Special rating, because it requires both NDE and wall-thickness/bolting documentation that the quote doesn't have on file
  • C.  Nothing about the MSS SP-66 citation — it remains the applicable design standard for power-plant piping
  • D.  Nothing at all — piping that is neither boiler-proper nor boiler-external isn't governed by any code
