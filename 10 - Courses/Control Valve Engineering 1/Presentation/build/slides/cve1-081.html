Direct- vs. Reverse-Acting Actuator Construction

  • Direct-acting — the baseline 12-part construction: diaphragm casing, diaphragm, diaphragm plate, actuator spring, spring seat/adjuster, yoke, stem connector, travel indicator disk and scale.
  • Reverse-acting — the same core parts with action reversed, plus four features the direct-acting design doesn't carry: seal bushing and O-rings, integrated handwheel-mounting bosses, an integral air passage, and an integral DVC6200 mounting pad.
