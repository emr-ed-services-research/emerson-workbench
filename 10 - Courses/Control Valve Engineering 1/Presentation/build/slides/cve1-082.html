Spring-and-Diaphragm — Sliding-Stem vs. Rotary Output

  • Sliding-stem — a typical spring-and-diaphragm actuator assembly, the linear output this design normally delivers straight through the stem.
  • Rotary — the same loading-pressure/diaphragm/spring construction, with a lever and shaft converting the actuator stem's linear motion into disk or ball rotation.
