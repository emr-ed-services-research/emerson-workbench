Piston Actuators — Single- vs. Double-Acting

  • Baseline (single-acting) — loading pressure connection, piston, piston seal, cylinder, cylinder closure seal, seal bushing, stem connector; air to one side of the piston only.
  • Double-acting — air supplied to both sides of the piston for maximum force in either direction, at the cost of needing a fail-safe accessory the single-acting design doesn't.
