Four Actuator Categories — Linkage Is the Real Difference

  • Four categories, same job — spring-and-diaphragm, piston, electric motor, electro-hydraulic — for either sliding-stem or rotary bodies; they differ only by linkage, not by power source.
  • Coupling matters where a linkage exists — a slotted connector on a milled flat is generally unsatisfactory, a pinned connection suits nominal torque, a splined, clamped connector eliminates lost motion and handles high torque.
