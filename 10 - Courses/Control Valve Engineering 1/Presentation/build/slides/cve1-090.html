Actuator Type Comparison — Cost, Thrust, Speed, Fail-Safe

Actuator TypeCostThrust, Stiffness & SpeedFail-SafeThrottling / Duty
Spring-and-diaphragm Lowest Limited output; larger size/weight for the thrust delivered Inherent Throttles without a positioner — start here first
Pneumatic piston Higher High thrust, compact & lightweight; fast stroking Needs an accessory or spring Needs a positioner to throttle
Electric motor — Compact, very stiff, high output; slow stroking No inherent fail-safe Traditionally on/off only; limited duty cycle
Electro-hydraulic Highest Highest output & stiffness; fast stroking — Best throttling performance; hardest maintenance
