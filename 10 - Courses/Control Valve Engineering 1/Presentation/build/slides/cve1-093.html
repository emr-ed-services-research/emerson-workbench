A Real Calculated Match, Not a Rule of Thumb

Total force required = A + B + C + D — unbalance force, seat load, packing friction, and any application-specific extra — summed and checked directly against a specific actuator's own diaphragm area and bench-set range. The valve has to close against the real differential pressure on the day it's called on, not the nameplate rating on the actuator's spec sheet.
