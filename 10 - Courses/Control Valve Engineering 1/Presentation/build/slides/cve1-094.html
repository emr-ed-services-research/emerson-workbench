Unbalance Force (A) — Reading Figure 5.2

  • 1Find the row for the actual port diameter, the column for the trim's balance type
  • 2Single-seated, flow-up, unbalanced trim — net unbalance area is simply the port area
  • 3Balanced trim — pressure routed to both sides of the plug cancels most of the force, but a small residual unbalance area remains; never assumed to be zero
  • 4Unbalance force A = net pressure differential × net unbalance area — the differential is usually the maximum upstream gauge pressure, unless back pressure at maximum inlet pressure is guaranteed
