Checking a Diaphragm Actuator Against 275 lbf Required

  1. Total force required = 275 lbf, from the A + B + C + D calculation just built
  2. Candidate: air-to-open diaphragm, 100 sq. in. effective area, bench set 6–15 psig, operating range 3–15 psig
  3. Precompression force = (6 psig bench-set floor − 3 psig operating-range floor) × 100 sq. in. = 3 psig × 100 sq. in. = 300 lbf
  4. Compare: 300 lbf exceeds the 275 lbf required — the actuator is an adequate selection, with 25 lbf of real margin
