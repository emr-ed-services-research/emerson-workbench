Piston Actuator Thrust

  1. Piston actuator, no spring — thrust = piston area × minimum supply pressure = available thrust
  2. No bench-set / precompression check — there is no spring range to overcome
  3. Keep units compatible: piston area in sq. in., pressure in psig, thrust in lbf
  4. Piston actuator WITH a spring is sized the same A + B + C + D way as the diaphragm actuator — the manufacturer publishes its own thrust, effective area, and spring data, plus a maximum-stem-load check
