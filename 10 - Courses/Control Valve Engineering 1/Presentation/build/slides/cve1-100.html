Rotary Sizing Matches Torque, Not Force

  • 1Rotary-valve actuator sizing matches torque, not force, to the actuator's output — the same fundamental process as the globe-valve force sizing just covered, applied to rotation instead of travel
  • 2Breakout Torque TB = A(ΔPshutoff) + B — the torque needed to start the valve moving from a dead stop against shutoff pressure
  • 3Dynamic Torque TD = C(ΔPeff) — the torque needed to keep it moving once underway
  • 4A, B, and C are real, valve-design-specific factors published per valve size and shaft diameter — not estimated
