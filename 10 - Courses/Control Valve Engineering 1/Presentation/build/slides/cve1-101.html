Working a Real Torque Case — 6 in. V-Notch Ball Valve

  1. 6 in. NPS V-notch ball valve, 1 in. composition-bearing shaft — A = 1.80, B = 500, C = 1.10 at 60° maximum rotation, maximum TD = 4,140 lbf·in
  2. Breakout torque: TB = 1.80(ΔPshutoff) + 500 lbf·in — the fixed B term alone is 500 lbf·in before any pressure differential is even applied
  3. Dynamic torque: TD = 1.10(ΔPeff) lbf·in, capped at this valve's own published maximum of 4,140 lbf·in regardless of what a larger ΔPeff would otherwise compute
  4. ΔPshutoff and ΔPeff come from the actual service, not the table — A, B, and C are the fixed, valve-specific factors the job's real pressure differentials get multiplied through
