Maximum Rotation — 90° Standard vs. Limited

Standard — 90°
The ball or disk rotates fully from closed to wide-open
Limited — 60–75°
A shorter rotation allows more initial spring compression, raising breakout torque — a real tradeoff. The lever's changing effective length across the shorter rotation is already built into the published torque factors, so A/B/C stay valid with no separate correction.
