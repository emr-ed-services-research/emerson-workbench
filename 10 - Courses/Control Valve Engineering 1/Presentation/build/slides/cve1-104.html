Sized Right Isn't the Same as Set Right

The force/torque calculation from the last module sizes the actuator correctly on paper — it says nothing about whether that actuator actually delivers its full rated travel once mounted. Bench set is the calibration step that closes that gap: an actuator sized right but bench-set wrong still short-strokes or over-strokes the valve, and neither the force check nor the torque check catches that failure mode. Sliding-stem valves depend on bench set most directly.
