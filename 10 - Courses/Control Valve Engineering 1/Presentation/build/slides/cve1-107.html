!Don't Overshoot Travel Past the Seat

Travel set wrong does not fail gracefully in either direction. Under-travel means the valve never reaches its designed flow rate no matter how hard the loop calls for more. Over-travel is worse: it can reduce the seat load actually delivered at the closed position — a real shutoff-capability loss — and damage the trim, in the extreme letting the plug bottom against the bonnet and pull the stem straight out of it. Sliding-stem valves demand more attention to travel setting than rotary valves.
