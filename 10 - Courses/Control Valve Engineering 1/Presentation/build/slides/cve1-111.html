Full-Stroke Speed vs. Small-Signal Response

Full-Stroke (100%)

Piston wins

A complete step-change stroke test rewards raw thrust — higher supply pressure drives a piston actuator through a full stroke fastest.
Small Signal (0.25–2%)

Spring-and-diaphragm wins

Real regulatory control makes small, continuous corrections, not full strokes. At that scale, documented industry research shows the result reverses: piston's extra friction (O-ring seals, more guide surfaces, alignment sensitivity, lubrication that degrades over time) specifically degrades small-signal responsiveness, even though it barely shows up on a full-stroke thrust test.
