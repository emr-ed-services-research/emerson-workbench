Electric vs. Electro-Hydraulic

Electric
A motor and gear train — the simpler, lower-cost path, economical only in small size ranges; typical fail action is lock-in-last-position, not a true fail-open or fail-closed.
Electro-Hydraulic
A motor pumps oil internally to a piston — high stiffness, analog-signal compatibility, excellent frequency response and positioning accuracy. Fail-safe is not inherent: a return spring or accumulator is needed, either self-contained (its own motor/pump/reservoir) or externally powered.
