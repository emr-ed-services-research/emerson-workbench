Friction and Price Aren’t the Whole Spec

A packing system chosen on stem friction and price alone can pass acceptance testing today and still surface as a fugitive-emissions compliance finding within a year, if the site’s actual emissions class was never checked against it. Every packing decision ahead in this module sits on two independent requirements — the service’s pressure/temperature envelope, and the emissions class the installation must meet — and satisfying one does not imply satisfying the other.
