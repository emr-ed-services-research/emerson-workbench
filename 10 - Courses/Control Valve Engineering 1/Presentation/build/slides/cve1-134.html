Bellows-Seal Bonnets — Welded-Leaf vs. Mechanically-Formed

  • 1Generic bellows-seal bonnet — isolates the stem from the process entirely; near-zero leakage (<1×10⁻⁶ cc/sec helium)
  • 2ENVIRO-SEAL production bonnet — a standard/environmental packing box is retained above the bellows as a catastrophic-failure backup only, never the primary seal
  • 3Welded-leaf element — shorter overall package height; manufacturing method can limit service life
  • 4Mechanically-formed element — taller; more repeatable manufacturing gives higher reliability
