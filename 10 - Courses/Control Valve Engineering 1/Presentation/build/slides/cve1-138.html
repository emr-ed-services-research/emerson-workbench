!Don’t Remove Packing for Storage — the Fix Is Worse Than the Risk

Graphite packing carries a real galvanic-corrosion risk to the stem under humid conditions with chlorides and oxygen present — a risk the number of variables involved makes near-impossible to predict in advance. Fisher mitigates it with a nonmetallic passivating inhibitor built into the base graphite material and a sacrificial zinc washer on every graphite packing system except nuclear service, but neither measure eliminates the risk outright. Shipping the valve with no packing installed at all would remove that risk — except someone could then put the valve into service without realizing it has no packing installed, which is exactly why every Fisher valve ships with packing already in, accepting the corrosion risk to avoid the bigger one.
