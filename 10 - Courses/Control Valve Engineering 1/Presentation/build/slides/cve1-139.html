!Specify Oxygen Service When You Order — Don’t Assume It’s Implied

Oxygen service — liquid or gaseous — needs one explicit precaution at the ordering stage: oxygen supports combustion of most lubricants and foreign material left in a packing system, so oxygen service must be specified by name when the packing or other equipment is ordered, so it ships already degreased. It is easy to miss precisely because it is a paperwork step at the ordering stage, not a hardware choice.
