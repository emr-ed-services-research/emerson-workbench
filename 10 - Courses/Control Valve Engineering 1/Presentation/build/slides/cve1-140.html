Selecting an End Connection

Connection typeRemovable without cutting the line?Range / limitation
Bolted flangeYesCovers the full working range most control valves are built for, to roughly 815°C or 15,000 psig depending on style
WeldedNo — must be cut outLeak-tight at any condition, cheapest first cost; a welded-only piping spec narrows the valve choice to sliding-stem bodies, and low-temperature composition trim must come out before welding to avoid heat damage

Either way: a dissimilar-metal flanged joint headed for extended outdoor storage carries the galvanic-corrosion risk just covered, and any packing ordered for oxygen service must be specified as such so it ships degreased — neither precaution is optional, and neither substitutes for the other.
