The Six-Step ISA/IEC Liquid Sizing Procedure

  • 1Specify the service conditions — q, P1, P2, T1, Gf, Pv, Pc
  • 2Pull the equation constant — N1 for volumetric flow, N6 for mass flow — from the equation-constants table
  • 3Determine Fp, the piping geometry factor, only if reducers, elbows, or tees are attached directly to the valve
  • 4Determine ΔPmax or qmax to check for choked flow
  • 5Solve for Cv using the lesser of the actual ΔP and ΔPmax
  • 6Select the valve size from the flow-coefficient table — repeat the whole loop if the calculated Cv exceeds the assumed valve’s own rated capacity

After Fisher Control Valve Sourcebook — Oil & Gas, Chapter 3 “Liquid Valve Sizing,” printed pp. 3-1–3-3 — the six steps as stated in the source, not a paraphrase.
