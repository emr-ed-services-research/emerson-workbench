Solving for Required Cv

  • 1Governing equation, volumetric flow: Cv = q ÷ (N1·Fp·√(ΔP/Gf)), N1 = 1.0 in gpm/psia units
  • 2Mass-flow form: substitutes N6 for N1 and w (mass flow rate) for q
  • 3Fp = 1.0 and drops out entirely when the valve is installed at line size — no reducers, elbows, or tees at its own connections
  • 4Two other flow-coefficient conventions convert directly off Cv: Kv = 0.865·Cv, Av = 2.40×10−5·Cv — same valve, same physical capacity, different unit system

After Fisher Control Valve Sourcebook — Oil & Gas, Chapter 3 “Liquid Valve Sizing,” printed p. 3-4 — the Cv equation and Kv/Av conversions transcribed from the source’s own step-5 equations.
