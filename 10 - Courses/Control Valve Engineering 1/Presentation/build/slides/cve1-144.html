Solving for Required Cv

Cv = q ÷ ( N1 · Fp ·√( ΔP ÷ Gf ))
Once solved, Kv = 0.865·Cv and Av = 2.40×10−5·Cv convert to the same valve’s capacity in other unit systems.
Cv is the valve’s flow capacity — the term this equation solves for.
