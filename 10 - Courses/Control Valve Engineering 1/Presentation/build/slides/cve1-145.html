The Piping Geometry Factor (Fp)

  • 1Fp only enters the equation when reducers, elbows, or tees are bolted directly onto the valve’s own inlet/outlet
  • 2Fp = [1 + (ΣK/N2)·(Cv/d²)²]−½, where ΣK = K1 + K2 + KB1 − KB2
  • 3KB1 and KB2 cancel when the piping is the same size on both sides of the valve — the common case
  • 4Between two identical concentric reducers: ΣK collapses to K1 + K2 = 1.5·(1 − d²/D²)², d = the valve’s nominal size, D = the line size

After Fisher Control Valve Sourcebook — Oil & Gas, Chapter 3, “Determining Piping Geometry Factor,” printed pp. 3-2–3-4 — the Fp equation and the concentric-reducer case transcribed as printed.
