The Piping Geometry Factor (Fp)

Fp = [1 + ( ΣK / N2 )·( Cv / d² )²]−½
Fp solves once ΣK, N2, and the valve’s own Cv are known.
Fp applies only when fittings are bolted directly to the valve. At line size, Fp = 1.0.

After Fisher Control Valve Sourcebook — Oil & Gas, Chapter 3, “Determining Piping Geometry Factor,” printed pp. 3-2–3-4.
