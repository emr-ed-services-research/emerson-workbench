  • A.  Use 45 psi — it’s the real service ΔP, so it’s always the correct value to plug in
  • B.  Use 30 psi — the flow is choked past that point, and using 45 psi instead would compute a Cv that’s too small, undersizing the valve
  • C.  Use 45 psi — ΔPmax only matters once the valve is installed, not during initial sizing
  • D.  Average the two values (37.5 psi) since both are real, measured pressure drops
