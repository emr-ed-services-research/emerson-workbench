Before You Calculate a Compressible Cv

A compressible-service Cv calculation that skips the choked-flow check can pass on paper and still never deliver rated flow in the field — once the pressure-drop ratio crosses the valve’s own critical value, opening it further buys nothing. The same ISA/IEC sizing logic from liquid sizing carries over, but two new gas-specific variables (molecular weight, specific-heat ratio) and a ratio-based choking test replace the liquid procedure’s absolute ΔPmax.
