What Compressible Sizing Adds

  • 1A gas’s density keeps changing as it drops pressure through the valve, unlike a liquid’s — so two new inputs join the service conditions: the gas’s molecular weight (M) and its specific-heat ratio (k)
  • 2Liquid sizing picks between two unit constants (N1 volumetric, N6 mass). Compressible sizing picks among four: N7 or N9 volumetric, N6 or N8 mass
  • 3Which half of each pair applies depends on which gas property was actually specified in the service conditions — specific gravity (Gg) or molecular weight (M)
