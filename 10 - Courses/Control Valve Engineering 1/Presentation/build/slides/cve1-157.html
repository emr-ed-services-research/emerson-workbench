The Expansion Factor (Y) and the Xtp Correction

  • 1Y = 1 − x ÷ (3·Fk·xTP) — the expansion factor drops linearly from 1.0 (no expansion) toward its 0.667 floor as x approaches the choked ratio
  • 2With reducers or other fittings at the valve’s own connections, xT is replaced by xTP = (xT/Fp²)·[1 + (xT·Ki/N5)·(Cv/d²)²]−1
  • 3Ki = K1 + KB1, the inlet head-loss coefficient — built from the same K-factor piping geometry m1’s Fp equation already uses
  • 4xTP folds the fittings’ own resistance into the choking threshold itself — a smaller valve in a larger line can choke at a different point than the flow-coefficient table’s rated xT alone would suggest
