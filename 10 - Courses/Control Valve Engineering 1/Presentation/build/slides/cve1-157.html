The Expansion Factor (Y) and the Xtp Correction

Y = 1 − x ÷ (3· Fk · xTP )
Once xTP is known (via Ki and the reducer geometry), Y solves directly, dropping from 1.0 toward its 0.667 floor as x approaches the choked ratio.
A liquid doesn’t expand as it drops pressure the way a gas does — liquid sizing has no equivalent term.
