Before You Blame the Valve

A control valve sized and selected correctly on paper can still fall short in the field — not because the valve is wrong, but because the piping immediately around it, and the line it’s dropped into, change what it can actually deliver. Piping arrangement measurably cuts real capacity independent of the valve’s own rating, and a line size mismatched to the valve carries its own strength and velocity risks — get either wrong, and the valve is what gets blamed.
