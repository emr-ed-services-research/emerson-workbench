Same Valve, Different Piping

Correct — 8-in Line, 6-in Valve

  • Block valves left at full 8-in line size
  • 15 diameters straight run upstream
  • 8 diameters straight run downstream
  • No elbows near the inlet

Same Valve, Wrong Arrangement

  • Block valves close-coupled at the valve’s own 6-in size
  • Close-coupled reducer creates a nozzle effect at the inlet
  • Real capacity is reduced — same hardware

Move the block valves out to line size and give the inlet its straight run, and the valve delivers what its sizing calculation promised — crowd the same valve against a reduced inlet and it can’t, even though nothing about the hardware changed.

After Fisher Power & Severe Service Sourcebook, Ch. 13, “Control Valve Piping,” printed pp. 13-1–13-2 — an illustrative 8-in/6-in installation applying the source’s own straight-run and block-valve-placement guidelines, not a source-reported case.
