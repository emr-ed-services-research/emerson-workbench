Two Rules That Don’t Agree

Rule 1 — Half the Pipe Size

Valve size ≥ ½ the line size

20-in line → 10-in floor

Adequate strength assurance on its own
Rule 2 — Two Sizes Below Line

Valve size ≥ line size − 2 nominal sizes

20-in line → 16-in floor

Also adequate on its own

After Fisher Power & Severe Service Sourcebook, Ch. 13, “Line Size Versus Valve Size,” printed p. 13-2 — the source’s own worked 20-in-line comparison, transcribed to show the two rules genuinely disagree. Component Index: pss-topic-line-size-vs-valve-size.
