Running Both Checks Together

Line-Size Check

  • Rule 1: valve ≥ half the line size
  • Rule 2: valve ≥ two sizes below the line size
  • Flag anything failing either rule — they can disagree

Velocity Check

  • Gas/vapor: Mach vs. 0.3 (Whisper Trim III) or 0.5 (Whisper Trim I)
  • Liquid, cage-guided: guide-wear/buffeting/erosion concerns largely don’t apply
  • Liquid, not cage-guided: they do — but clean-fluid velocity alone still isn’t grounds for an erosion limit

Worked: an 18-in line fitted with a 9-in cage-guided valve in clean liquid service passes Rule 1 but fails Rule 2 — and because the valve is cage-guided, velocity isn’t the deciding factor here, so the line-size disagreement is the real open question, not an assumed erosion risk.

After Fisher Power & Severe Service Sourcebook, Ch. 13, “Line Size Versus Valve Size” (p. 13-2) and “Velocity Limitations in Control Valves” (pp. 13-2–13-3) — both rules and both velocity criteria applied together to an illustrative installation, not a source-reported case.
