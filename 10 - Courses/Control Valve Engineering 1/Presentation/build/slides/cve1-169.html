!Pull Elastomeric Trim Before PWHT

If post-weld heat treatment (PWHT) will be performed, remove all trim parts first — PWHT holds the welded area at up to roughly 1400°F for several hours, and even though the heat is applied locally, it conducts back into the valve body. The same caution applies during preheat and the welding pass itself whenever the joint temperature runs close to or above a part’s own limit, since elastomeric and plastic parts (O-rings, seal rings, backup rings, packing rings, gaskets) have lower temperature limits than the surrounding metal. When in doubt, remove the part — leaving elastomeric or plastic trim in place through PWHT, or through a preheat/interpass temperature it can’t tolerate, damages trim that was never defective to begin with.
