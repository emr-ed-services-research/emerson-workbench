!Replace Gaskets and Packing at Reassembly

Once a valve must be disassembled — to remove trim for welding/PWHT, or to install flushing trim — have a full extra set of gaskets and packing ready before reassembly. Gaskets should always be replaced when a valve has been disassembled, and packing should be replaced too: pulling the stem out of the packing box risks damaging packing that still looks reusable. Reusing packing or gaskets can fail later as a leak, and the resulting damage can erode the gasket surface, the packing box bore, or the stem itself — turning a routine disassembly into a repeat failure.
