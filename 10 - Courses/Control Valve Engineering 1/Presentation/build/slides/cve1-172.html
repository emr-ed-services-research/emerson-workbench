Check Your Knowledge — Welding Procedure & Commissioning Flush

A welded-end valve with drilled-hole trim is scheduled for post-weld heat treatment (PWHT) after both butt welds are made, and the plant plans a water flush through the piping before startup. To save an extra teardown, the crew leaves the real trim installed through PWHT, planning to swap in sacrificial flushing trim just before the flush. What is wrong with this plan?

  • A.  Nothing — PWHT only heats the weld joint itself, so parts already installed elsewhere in the valve are not at risk
  • B.  PWHT holds the area at up to roughly 1400°F for hours, and that heat conducts back into the body — the real trim should have been removed before PWHT, not after
  • C.  The flushing trim should be installed permanently in place of the real trim, since it is already handling the flush
  • D.  The plan is fine as long as the gaskets and packing are replaced, not reused, at final reassembly

B · Remove trim before PWHT, not after
