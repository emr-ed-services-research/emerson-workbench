Correctly Sized Isn’t the Same as Counted

A packing system or an actuator choice you already know how to size correctly doesn’t automatically count as “sustainability content” on a customer’s books — it only counts once you can say which of three emissions-accounting scopes it lands in. Most operators can report Scope 1 and Scope 2 with real confidence; Scope 3 is typically their largest single contributor, and the one most haven’t fully measured yet. Get the scope right, and a correctly-selected valve becomes part of the business case for the better, costlier option. Get it wrong, and the same hardware choice never shows up on the account it actually belongs on.
