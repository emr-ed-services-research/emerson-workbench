Three Valve-Related Emissions, Three Scopes

  • Packing gland fugitive leak at the stem, or a positioner’s continuous instrument-air bleed — Scope 1, direct: happens at the company’s own site, the same category the source names for natural-gas actuation.
  • Electric actuator’s continuous draw on the plant’s own purchased grid power — Scope 2, indirect: the emissions occur at the generating plant, not on-site.
  • A customer’s own purchased replacement trim or actuator kit — Scope 3, indirect value-chain: the manufacturing, materials, and transportation footprint already embedded in that hardware before it ever reaches the customer’s site.

Same mechanism, three different accounts — where an emission or a purchase lands depends on how directly the company controls the source, not on whether valve hardware is involved.
