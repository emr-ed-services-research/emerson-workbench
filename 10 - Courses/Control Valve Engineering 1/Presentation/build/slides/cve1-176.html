Two Pathways, One Efficiency Lever

  • 1Mitigation — deploying renewable resources and efficiency improvements to cut emissions at carbon-intensive industrial processes directly.
  • 2CCUS (Carbon Capture, Utilization & Storage) — extracting point-source carbon emissions and sequestering them underground; can remove 90–99% of a facility’s carbon emissions — the only technology group that both reduces emissions directly and removes CO2 already in the atmosphere.
  • 3Energy efficiency — the lever behind both: only about one-third of today’s primary energy actually performs its intended function; the rest is lost to heat escape, braking, line losses, and unoptimized processes.
