Greening OF, BY, and WITH

CategoryWhat it meansReal examples
Greening OF — internal initiativesA company’s own actions to cut its own GHG footprint, plus engaging upstream suppliers to reduce emissions in what they supplyLED retrofits, automated lighting shutdown, compressed-air leak detection, tuned chilled-water scheduling
Greening BY — products & services soldWhat a company sells that helps its customers decarbonize — the category where control-valve hardware choices are themselves the sustainability offeringControl systems, software, electric actuation, environmental packing, valve condition monitoring
Greening WITH — industry-wide collaborationNo single stakeholder — government, business, community, or academia — can manage the low-carbon transition aloneIndustry forums, research-institution partnerships, multi-stakeholder alliances
