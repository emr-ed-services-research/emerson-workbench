Actuator and Packing Choices on the Customer’s ESG Scorecard

Actuator electrification and fugitive-emissions reduction are named line items on a customer’s own emerging-technology list — and the emissions reduction they deliver reports directly into that customer’s Environmental ESG pillar, which is what turns a correctly-selected, costlier packing or actuator option into a business case an engineer can actually make.
