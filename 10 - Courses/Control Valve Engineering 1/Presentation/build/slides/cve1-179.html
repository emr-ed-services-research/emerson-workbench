Check Your Knowledge — Emissions Scopes & Decarbonization Frameworks

An operator retrofits a natural-gas-actuated valve with a low-power electric actuator to cut its own site’s fugitive methane venting. From the operator’s own perspective — not the actuator manufacturer’s — which scope does the eliminated venting fall under, and which “Greening” category does the operator’s own retrofit decision belong to?

  • A.  Scope 2; Greening WITH — the emissions were indirect and purchased, and multiple stakeholders collaborated on the retrofit
  • B.  Scope 1; Greening OF — the venting was the operator’s own direct, on-site emission, and the retrofit is the operator’s own internal action to cut its own footprint
  • C.  Scope 3; Greening BY — the venting carries the retrofit kit’s own embedded manufacturing footprint, and offering electric actuation is Greening BY
  • D.  Scope 1; Greening BY — the venting is direct, and selling electric actuation to operators is exactly what Greening BY names

B · Scope 1; Greening OF
