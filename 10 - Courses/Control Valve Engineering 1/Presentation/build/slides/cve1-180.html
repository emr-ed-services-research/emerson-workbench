More Sustainable Isn’t the Same as Net-Zero

You already made two hardware calls this course would call “more sustainable” — an electric actuator over a gas-actuated one (Chapter 6), a lower-fugitive-emissions packing system (Chapter 7). Neither one, on its own, gets an operator to net-zero. A company can offset 100% of its reported emissions every single year and call itself carbon-neutral without ever having reduced what it actually emits. Before either hardware choice counts toward a real net-zero target, you need the distinction the source itself draws between the two claims — and what a target-setting process actually has to show to earn the word “net-zero.”
