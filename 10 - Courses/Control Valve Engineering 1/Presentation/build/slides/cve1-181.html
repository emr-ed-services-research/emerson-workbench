Carbon-Neutral vs. Net-Zero

Carbon-Neutral
Any CO2 a company’s activities release into the atmosphere is balanced by an equivalent amount being removed — offsetting is permitted, and expected.
Mechanism: balance the footprint after the fact.
Net-Zero
The activity releases net-zero carbon emissions in the first place — no carbon needs to be captured or offset, because none was emitted to begin with.
Mechanism: never emit it to begin with.

After Control Valve Handbook, 6th ed., §11.12 (Commonly Used Terms in Sustainability), pp. 247–248 — verbatim source definitions: “Total Net-Zero = Net Zero.” No figure registered. Component Index: cvh-topic-carbon-neutral-vs-net-zero.
