Setting a Real Net-Zero Target

  • Baseline analysis — an in-depth analysis of total emissions across Scopes 1, 2, and 3 (Module 1) fixes a baseline year, then a target year to reach net-zero from it.
  • Aligned to current climate science — the Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) Net-Zero Standard, described as the world’s leading organization driving adoption of science-based targets.
  • ≥90% absolute reduction — a robust net-zero design requires at least a 90% absolute reduction in greenhouse-gas emissions from the baseline; high-quality offsetting is permitted only for whatever residual can’t otherwise be abated — never as a stand-in for the reduction itself.
  • Monitored yearly — progress is measured every year, not declared once and left alone.
