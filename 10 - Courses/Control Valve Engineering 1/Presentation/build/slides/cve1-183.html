Two Real, Hardware-Traceable Methane Sources

  • Remote oil-and-gas production & pipelines — natural gas itself is often the motive force for instrumentation and valve actuation. Methane is vented with each valve stroke, or bled continuously from the instrumentation, all day, every day the station runs.
  • Chemical service — control valves and pumps emit through packing and seal leaks — the identical fugitive-emissions path Chapter 7’s own packing-selection criteria already exist to control, now counted here as a real, quantifiable Scope 1 methane source in its own right.
