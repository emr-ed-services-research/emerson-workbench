Four Fixes, Two Sources You Already Know

  • Electric actuator retrofit (Chapter 6) — replacing a natural-gas-actuated valve, often sold as a retrofit kit, delivers a dramatic reduction in methane venting.
  • Low-bleed pneumatic instrumentation — cuts the continuous-bleed source without a full electrification retrofit.
  • Very-low-power electric alternatives — can run on a small solar system’s own output, reaching a remote station with no grid connection at all.
  • Improved packing designs (Chapter 7) — close the seal-leak path in chemical service.

None of these four is a new competency — they’re this course’s own actuator and packing selections, read as decarbonization levers.
