Check Your Knowledge — Net-Zero Targets & the Valve’s Contribution

A remote-pipeline operator has purchased enough carbon offsets to cover 100% of its reported emissions for the fifth year running, while its unmanned stations keep actuating valves on vented natural gas exactly as before. An engineer proposes replacing those gas actuators with low-power electric units and improving packing at a chemical-service unit with a known stem leak. Which statement is correct?

  • A.  The operator is already net-zero, since 100% of its reported emissions are offset every year — the engineer’s proposal is unnecessary
  • B.  The operator is carbon-neutral, not net-zero — it has offset its footprint but never reduced it; the engineer’s proposal would cut real Scope 1 emissions and move the operator toward an actual net-zero target
  • C.  The operator is neither carbon-neutral nor net-zero until it clears the 90% absolute-reduction floor, and the electric-actuator retrofit is itself a Scope 3 reduction once installed
  • D.  The packing fix addresses a real emission, but since the actuator and packing were purchased from a vendor, both fixes count as the operator’s Scope 3, not Scope 1

B · Carbon-neutral, not net-zero
