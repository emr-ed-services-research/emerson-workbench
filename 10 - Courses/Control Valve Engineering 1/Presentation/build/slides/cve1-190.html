Signature Series Testing — Building the Baseline

  1. Series 1 — record the baseline: instrument configuration, a 50%-travel status check, the valve spec sheet, and the valve's own signature, error-band curve, and drive signal, swept −5% to 105% input
  2. Series 2 — add a performance step test; expand the status check to 0/25/50/75/100% travel
  3. Series 3 — a custom Series 1/2 test, with adjustable end points and scan times for the application
  4. Data Comparison — once in service, re-run the same tests in ValveLink and compare against the factory baseline to catch developing problems early
