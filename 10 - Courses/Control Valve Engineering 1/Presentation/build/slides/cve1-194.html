Check Your Knowledge — Diagnosing Valve-Level Causes

A loop is hunting at low flow. The valve's own Response Time Summary shows a small, consistent Td and T63 across every tested step size and direction — the valve moves promptly every time. But a ValveLink Total Scan overlay shows the current signature's span is visibly wider than the factory baseline across the whole stroke. What is the most likely cause, and what actually fixes it?

  • A.  Deadband from linkage backlash — replace the linkage
  • B.  Slow dynamic response — resize the positioner or actuator
  • C.  Increased friction in the valve assembly — service it; retuning will not fix a widened span
  • D.  A controller-tuning mismatch — retune to a faster time constant
