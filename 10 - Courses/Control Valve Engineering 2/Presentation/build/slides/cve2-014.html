Check Your Knowledge: Noise & Cavitation Mitigation Selection

A service is confirmed cavitation-limited, not noise-limited — local pressure is dropping to vapor pressure inside the valve. Which mitigation addresses the actual failure mode?

  • A.  Anti-noise trim (multi-slot cage)
  • B.  Inline diffuser
  • C.  Multi-stage anti-cavitation trim
  • D.  Inline silencer

C · It stages the pressure drop internally so no single stage's local pressure falls to vapor pressure — the other three are noise treatments (source or path-side) and don't stop cavitation.
