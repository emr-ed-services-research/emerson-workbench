Check Your Knowledge: Severe-Service Design Justification

A service will run at -115°C at the packing box. Which design responds to this specific threshold?

  • A.  Low-flow Cv trim (tight tolerance)
  • B.  Cryogenic extension bonnet
  • C.  DST-style multi-stage anti-cavitation trim
  • D.  ASME Section III Class 1 construction

B · -115°C is past the -101°C/-150°F frost point — the extension bonnet keeps the packing box above that number so atmospheric moisture can't freeze and tear the packing.
