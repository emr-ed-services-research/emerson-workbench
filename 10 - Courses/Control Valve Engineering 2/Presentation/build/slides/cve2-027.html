Check Your Knowledge: Desuperheater Design Selection

A service needs 40:1 rangeability at a minimum steam velocity down to 12 ft/s, on a pipe large enough for an insertion-style design. Which design meets this window?

  • A.  Fixed-geometry nozzle (5:1, 25-30 ft/s)
  • B.  Variable-geometry nozzle (20:1, 25-30 ft/s)
  • C.  Steam-atomized (50:1, down to ~10 ft/s)
  • D.  Geometry-assisted wafer (20:1, small pipe only)

C · It's the only design whose stated rangeability (50:1) and minimum velocity (~10 ft/s) both clear the requirement — at the cost of needing a separate atomizing-steam supply.
