Check Your Knowledge: Turbine-Protection Loop Architecture

A proposed turbine-bypass loop achieves Class V shutoff but strokes fully in 6 seconds. Does this loop meet the turbine-protection requirement?

  • A.  Yes — Class V is the harder requirement, so it's sufficient alone
  • B.  No — both Class V AND 2-4s stroke response are required together
  • C.  Yes — any stroke time under 10 seconds is acceptable for turbine protection
  • D.  No — Class V shutoff is not actually relevant to turbine protection

B · A fast actuator with the wrong shutoff class, or a tight-shutoff design too slow to stroke, both fail the real turbine-protection task — 6 seconds misses the stated 2-4 second bar.
