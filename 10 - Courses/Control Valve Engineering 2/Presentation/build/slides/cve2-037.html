Check Your Knowledge: Standards Scheme Reconciliation

A Zone 1 gas application requires Group IIB equipment. A proposed substitute is IEC-rated IIA. Is this substitution valid?

  • A.  Yes — IIA and IIB are interchangeable tiers
  • B.  No — IIA-marked equipment is suitable only for IIA, not IIB
  • C.  Yes, but only if the ATEX rating is 2G
  • D.  Cannot be determined without the EPL/Zone table

B · The hierarchy is asymmetric and one-directional — IIC covers IIB and IIA, IIB covers IIA, but IIA covers only IIA. Reading it backward approves an unsafe substitution.
