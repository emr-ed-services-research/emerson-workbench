Check Your Knowledge: Isolation Valve Variant Justification

A large gate valve is reported chattering at partial closure. Which variant addresses this specific failure mode?

  • A.  Solid wedge gate valve
  • B.  Flexible wedge disk (with guides)
  • C.  Double disk gate valve
  • D.  Flexible split-wedge gate valve

B · The flexible wedge disk's guides on larger sizes exist specifically to prevent chattering — the failure mode the rigid solid wedge is more prone to at that size. (Content credited to IPT's Pipe Trades Handbook by Robert A. Lee.)
