Check Your Knowledge: Decarbonization Technology Tradeoff

Offshore wind and actuator electrification are both real, currently deployed decarbonization technologies. Which is genuinely control-valve-relevant to a sizing engineer's own work?

  • A.  Offshore wind — it addresses Scope 2 emissions
  • B.  Actuator electrification — it changes what an engineer specifies on the valve
  • C.  Both, equally
  • D.  Neither — both are outside engineering scope

B · Real but not my job vs. real and my job is the actual judgment — offshore wind is a genuine decarbonization technology with no bearing on control valve engineering work at all.
