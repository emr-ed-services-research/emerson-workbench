Check Your Knowledge: Onshore/Offshore Production

A slug-catcher control valve spec calls out a 3000 psig raw wellhead inlet and cites erosive, particulate-laden flow. What does this tell you about the facility?

  • A.  It's an offshore topsides facility — high-pressure duty is typical there
  • B.  It's an onshore well site — the raw, erosive, high-pressure inlet condition is the onshore-specific driver, not a weight/footprint constraint
  • C.  The facility type can't be determined from pressure and erosion alone
  • D.  It must be a slug catcher on an FPSO, since only offshore units have slug catchers

B · Raw, erosive, high-pressure wellhead duty is the onshore-specific constraint; offshore's binding constraint is weight/footprint/access, not inlet severity.
