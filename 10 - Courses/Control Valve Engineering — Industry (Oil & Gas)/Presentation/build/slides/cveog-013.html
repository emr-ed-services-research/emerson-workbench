Check Your Knowledge: Natural Gas Treatment

A treatment-train valve is specified for a duty point downstream of the amine contactor, where H2S recovered from the gas stream is being converted to elemental sulfur. Which stage's duty does this valve actually serve?

  • A.  Amine treatment — it's still handling the same acid gas
  • B.  Dehydration — anything downstream of amine treatment is drying
  • C.  Sulfur recovery — this is the Claus-process stage that closes the loop on what amine treatment removed
  • D.  Tail gas treatment — any leftover H2S handling is tail gas

C · Converting recovered H2S to elemental sulfur is sulfur recovery's own duty, a real downstream consequence of amine treatment's upstream removal step.
