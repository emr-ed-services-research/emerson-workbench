Distillation-Column Control

The depropanizer repeats the deethanizer's pattern one stage down: heat-pump compressor (station 1) with a recycle path (2), a heat exchanger shared with the deethanizer (2, 4), a reflux accumulator (3), reflux pumps (5, 6), and a hot-oil reboiler (7) handing the remaining liquid to the debutanizer (8) — recognizing the reflux/reboiler pattern once means recognizing it here without re-deriving it.
