A pipeline valve's spec includes a note that upstream metering equipment requires flow conditions to hold within a tight tolerance for a billable measurement. What is this valve's actual binding constraint?

  • A.  Line pressure management, same as a pump station
  • B.  Measurement accuracy — a constraint pump-station pressure-management duty doesn't share
  • C.  Erosion resistance, same as an onshore wellhead choke
  • D.  Weight and footprint, same as an offshore topsides valve

B · A metering station's duty is inseparable from measurement accuracy — distinct from a pump station's pressure-profile duty.
