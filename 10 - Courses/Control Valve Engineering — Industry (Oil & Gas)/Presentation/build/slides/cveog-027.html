Check Your Knowledge: Natural Gas Storage

A natural-gas storage facility's spec has no water-injection or brine-disposal valves at all. What does this tell you about its formation type?

  • A.  It must be a salt-cavern site with a simplified design
  • B.  It's a depleted-reservoir or aquifer site — those formation types genuinely have fewer valve stations, not a simplified version of the salt-cavern set
  • C.  The formation type can't be inferred from valve count
  • D.  It's missing required hardware regardless of formation type

B · Only a salt-cavern site needs the water-injection/brine-disposal loop to leach storage volume; the other two formation types never have it.
