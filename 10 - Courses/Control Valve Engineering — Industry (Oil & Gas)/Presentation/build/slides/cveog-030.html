Check Your Knowledge: LNG Liquefaction

CVE2's severe-service example uses a −101°C frost point. LNG liquefaction cools gas to −161°C. What has to change in the material-selection judgment between the two?

  • A.  Nothing — colder is just a more extreme version of the same severe-service judgment, no real change
  • B.  The judgment doesn't transfer at all — LNG needs an entirely different engineering discipline
  • C.  The same material-selection judgment applies, but at a materially colder real number that a learner shouldn't assume tops out near CVE2's example
  • D.  Only the trim material changes; body material selection stays identical regardless of temperature

C · The fundamental judgment transfers, but LNG's real −161°C is materially colder than CVE2's example — assuming the example is the ceiling would be a real mistake.
