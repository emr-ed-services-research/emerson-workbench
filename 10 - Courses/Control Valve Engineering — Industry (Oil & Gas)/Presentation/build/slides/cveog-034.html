Check Your Knowledge: LNG Receiving Terminals

A receiving terminal and a liquefaction plant both operate in the same cryogenic temperature regime. What genuinely carries over between them, and what doesn't?

  • A.  Everything carries over — they're the same process run in reverse
  • B.  Nothing carries over — a receiving terminal shares no engineering ground with liquefaction
  • C.  The cryogenic material-selection judgment carries over; the process function itself (ship unloading/vapor return/regasification vs. a refrigerant cycle) is genuinely different
  • D.  Only the vapor-return duty point carries over; material selection has to be redone from scratch

C · The temperature constraint and its material-selection judgment transfer across both LNG processes; the process itself differs.
