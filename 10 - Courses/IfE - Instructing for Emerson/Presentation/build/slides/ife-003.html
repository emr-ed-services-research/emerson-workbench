An instructor spends their break before class checking tools, staging consumables, and confirming the training area meets the safety brief — before the first student arrives. Which tenet are they demonstrating?

  • A.  Context to Content
  • B.  Training Objectives
  • C.  Student Preparation
  • D.  Acuminating Instructor Skills

C. Student Preparation — preparing the learner and the environment before they arrive.
