Bench Set

  • Bench-set line — 0 % to full rated travel
  • Bench-set range

Bench set is the diaphragm-pressure range that strokes the actuator through its rated travel on the bench — with no process or valve forces acting. The spring rate is picked for the service and the shutoff class wanted.
