Check Your Knowledge: Fail-Safe Action

A process needs its control valve to drive fully open if instrument air is lost. The valve body is push-down-to-close. Which actuator action gives that fail-safe result?

  • A.  Direct-acting — the spring drives the stem up, lifting the plug off the PDTC seat
  • B.  Reverse-acting — the spring drives the stem down onto the PDTC seat
  • C.  Either — body orientation alone sets fail mode
  • D.  Neither — a PDTC body can only fail closed

A — direct-acting fails up; on a push-down-to-close body a rising stem opens the valve.
