Set the Bench Set — Actuator Off the Valve

  1. Actuator off the valve, stems unconnected — no packing friction acting.
  2. Raise loading pressure to the lower bench-set value. Turn the spring adjuster in or out of the yoke until the stem first moves exactly at that pressure.
  3. Raise to the upper bench-set pressure; the stem strokes fully. Mark the stem at each end of travel.
  4. Measure between the marks — it must equal the nameplate rated travel. If short or long, re-adjust and repeat.
