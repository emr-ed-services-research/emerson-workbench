On the Valve, Friction Shifts the Curve Right

  • Off the valve — friction-free
  • On the valve — with packing friction
  • 1Upper bench set (e.g. 11 psig)
  • 2Bench set + friction ÷ diaphragm area

On the valve, full rated travel no longer happens at the bench-set pressure — it happens at bench set plus the friction force divided by the effective diaphragm area. That is why the bench set is set and verified off the valve, where friction is not in play.
