Check: Short Travel Off the Valve

You are bench-setting a Fisher™ 657 with the actuator off the valve. At the lower bench-set pressure the stem first moves right on the mark, but at the upper bench-set pressure the travel between your marks is less than the rated travel on the nameplate. What is the most likely cause?

  • A.  Packing friction is still acting on the actuator
  • B.  The wrong spring is installed, or the spring adjuster is set wrong
  • C.  The diaphragm is ruptured
  • D.  The loading-pressure gauge is reading high

B. The wrong spring is installed, or the spring adjuster is set wrong. The actuator is off the valve, so friction (A) is not in play; a ruptured diaphragm (C) would not hold pressure to stroke at all; a high-reading gauge (D) would shift both pressures, not shorten the travel between them.
