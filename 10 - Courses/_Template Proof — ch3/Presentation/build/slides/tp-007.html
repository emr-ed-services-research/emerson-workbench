Stem Connector — Thread Engagement

  1. Open the two-piece stem connector — one threaded bore takes the actuator stem, the other takes the valve stem
  2. Engage each stem by at least one stem diameter of thread — too little strips the threads or mis-sets travel
  3. Make fine travel adjustments at the locknut and jam nut
