Check Your Knowledge 3: Fisher™ 657

A 657 is still mounted on the valve and you need to replace its diaphragm. Before you loosen the diaphragm-casing cap screws, which step protects you from the stored spring force?

  • A.  Disconnect the stem connector
  • B.  Back the spring adjuster out until all spring compression is relieved
  • C.  Apply full loading pressure to the diaphragm
  • D.  Tighten the yoke locknut against the bonnet

B. Back the spring adjuster out until all spring compression is relieved
