Diaphragm-Casing Bolt Torque

  1. Bolts go on clean and dry — no lubricant.
  2. Round 1: snug the four bolts 90° apart (①–④), then criss-cross the rest, to 13 N·m (10 lbf·ft).
  3. Round 2: same order, to 27 N·m (20 lbf·ft). Do not exceed 27 N·m or the diaphragm is damaged.
  4. Finish with one circular check pass, bolt to bolt.
