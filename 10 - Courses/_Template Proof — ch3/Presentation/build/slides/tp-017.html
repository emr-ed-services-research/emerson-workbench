Bench Set — Verify Off the Valve

  1. Actuator off the valve, stem at the top of its travel — no packing friction.
  2. Raise to the lower bench-set pressure from the nameplate; mark the stem where it first moves.
  3. Raise to the upper bench-set pressure; the stem extends fully. Mark the stem again.
  4. Measure between the marks — it must equal the nameplate rated travel.
