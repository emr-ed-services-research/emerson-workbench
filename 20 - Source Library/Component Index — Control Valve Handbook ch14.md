## Components

**None catalogued.** Chapter 14 is pipe/flange dimensional reference data
(pipe engagement lengths, carbon/alloy/stainless pipe schedules, American
pipe flange dimensions, and ten cast-steel flange-standard tables for
PN 10 through PN 400) presented as numbered-section data grids with no
individually-captioned "Figure 14.N" or "Table 14.N" objects. There is
nothing meeting the figures-only rule's definition of a component to
record.
