# Component Index — Fisher EZ easy-e Control Valve Instruction Manual

Standing full-chapter cataloging pass (document grain), part of the "index
all Technical Publications manuals" directive (Franz, 2026-09-19). Matches
the rigor of every prior pass: all 36 pages rendered as images (Poppler,
130dpi) and visually inspected — no figure catalogued from text extraction
alone.

House structure: Introduction (Scope of Manual, Description,
Specifications, Educational Services), Installation, Maintenance (Packing
Lubrication, Packing Maintenance/Replacing Packing, Trim
Maintenance/Disassembly/Lapping Metal Seats/Assembly, ENVIRO-SEAL Bellows
Seal and Bonnet), Parts Ordering, Parts Kits, Parts List — flat named
headers, confirmed against the manual's own real Contents page.

**Real page range:** 36 PDF pages, no front-matter offset — printed page
number equals PDF page number throughout. Figures numbered flatly (Figure
1 through Figure 15), confirmed against the manual's own captions.

**Note on valve size:** EZ is the smallest-bore valve in the easy-e family
catalogued this pass — NPS 1/2 through 4 (vs. ES/ED/ET's larger ranges),
with quick-change trim as its defining feature (post guiding, for
non-lubricating/viscous/hard-to-handle fluids). Its packing figures stop at
19.1mm (3/4in) stem, one size smaller than the other three manuals' 31.8mm
(1-1/4in) maximum — a real, confirmed difference in scope, not an
inconsistency.
