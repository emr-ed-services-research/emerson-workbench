# Industry Handbooks

The **Fisher Control Valve Sourcebook** series held in the Source Library, in
`20 - Source Library/Industry Specific Sourcebooks/`. Each sourcebook pairs a
common control-valve fundamentals section with an industry-specific section that
walks the processes of that industry and identifies the control valves used at
each point, along with the problem valves to watch.

Four files are held. "Power & Severe Service" is a **single combined
sourcebook**, not two, so it covers both the power-generation applications and
the severe-service topics.

All four share the same two-part shape:

- **Section I — Control Valve Sizing and Selection:** valve selection, actuator
  selection, liquid valve sizing, gas valve sizing, control valve noise,
  cavitation and flashing. (Read this first if control-valve terminology isn't
  familiar.)
- **Section II — Industry applications:** one chapter per process area, each
  with a process description, a functional process drawing, the Fisher valves to
  consider and their function, and notes on troublesome valves.

### Oil & Gas

Section II — Oil and Gas Control Valve Applications: onshore oil & gas
production (Ch 7), offshore oil & gas production (Ch 8), natural gas treatment
(Ch 9), fractionation (Ch 10), oil and gas transportation (Ch 11), natural gas
storage (Ch 12), LNG liquefaction (Ch 13), LNG receiving terminals (Ch 14). A
value-chain flow diagram in the preface maps the chapters onto the wellhead →
treatment → transportation → LNG path.

### Power & Severe Service

Section II covers power-generation applications — boiler feedwater, steam
conditioning and desuperheating, turbine bypass, combined-cycle / HRSG,
geothermal, and cogeneration — together with severe-service topics
(anti-cavitation and high-pressure-drop trim, noise abatement), materials
guidelines, a codes-and-standards overview, piping and installation guidelines,
and control valve performance / diagnostics.

### Refining

Section II walks the refinery unit by unit: crude (atmospheric) distillation
and the desalter, vacuum distillation, hydrotreating, catalytic reforming,
fluid catalytic cracking (FCC), hydrocracking, coking and visbreaking,
isomerization, alkylation, amine treating / sulfur recovery / tail-gas
treating, and product blending and the gas plant. A refinery block-flow diagram
in the introduction ties the units together.

### Pulp & Paper

Section II — chapters 7–18: steam conditioning (Ch 7), process overview (Ch 8),
pulping (Ch 9), batch digesters (Ch 10A), continuous digesters (Ch 10B), black
liquor evaporators / concentrators (Ch 11), kraft recovery boiler (Ch 12),
recausticizing and lime recovery (Ch 13), bleaching and brightening (Ch 14),
stock preparation (Ch 15), wet end chemistry (Ch 16), paper machine (Ch 17),
power and recovery boiler (Ch 18). Each chapter flags problem valves — erosion,
stiction, linkage deadband, packing leakage, material incompatibility.
