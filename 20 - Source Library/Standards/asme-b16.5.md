## What rests on it

Nothing yet. The entry exists because the component catalogue design names
B16.5 as the authority for its `flange` class, and the register's own check
failed the moment that citation appeared with no entry behind it — which is
the check doing its job, on the same day the design argued for it.

## What was deliberately not taken

All of it. Flanges are not in any current rig: level 1 is small-bore, and the
bench's DVC6200 connections are threaded. This is registered ahead of first
use rather than after it.
