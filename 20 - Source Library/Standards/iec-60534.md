## What rests on it

Cited in CVE1 alongside `ANSI/FCI 70-2` as its harmonised equivalent for seat
leakage (Part 4).

**Nothing yet rests on Part 8-3.** It is registered ahead of use so that the
noise work has somewhere to point.

## Notes for the work that will use it

- **Clause 6 is the anti-noise trim taxonomy** — single-stage multipath,
  single-path multistage, multipath multistage. That is the standard's own
  classification of the trim family Whisper belongs to, and the right spine for
  explaining it.
- **Clause 8 is where manufacturer test data legitimately enters**, replacing
  the generic acoustical efficiency with measured factors.
- **Annex A is the grading target.** A noise implementation should be graded
  against its worked examples the way the liquid sizing was graded against the
  Control Valve Handbook's printed example.
- Emerson's own `109 Control Valve Noise Basics` (GB109, SDSPublic, 2024-03)
  sets out the seven-step method citing this standard, and carries a worked
  example. Internal, shareable, and the better teaching source.

## What was deliberately not taken

The equations, the coefficient tables and Annex A's worked numbers. Reading the
contents page establishes WHAT the method contains and WHERE each piece lives;
it does not license transcribing it, and no implementation should begin by
quoting from here.
