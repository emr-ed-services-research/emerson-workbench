# ISO 15926 — Integration of life-cycle data for process plants including oil and gas production facilities

**Publisher:** ISO
**Edition on hand / cited:** unverified
**Holding:** `publisher-licensed`

## Access

ISO enterprise set. Not opened.

## What has been read

Nothing.

## What rests on it

Nothing implemented. It supplies the vocabulary DEXPI builds on, and DEXPI's port-based connection model is what the Process Core netlist was designed against — see `00 - Project/Process Core — Architecture & Build Plan.md`. The netlist follows DEXPI's SHAPE, not this standard's data model.

## What was deliberately not taken

The data model itself. The netlist is deliberately not an ISO 15926 or DEXPI serialisation — it borrows the idea that components connect through named ports rather than coordinates, and nothing more.
