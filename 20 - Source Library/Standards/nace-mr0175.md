# NACE MR0175 — Petroleum and natural gas industries — Materials for use in H2S-containing environments

**Publisher:** NACE / ISO (published jointly as ISO 15156)
**Edition on hand / cited:** ANSI/NACE MR0175-2021 / ISO 15156:2020, Parts 1-3
**Holding:** `publisher-licensed`

## Access

NACE is in Emerson's enterprise set; the ISO 15156 joint publication is in the
ISO set. Not opened -- but **located exactly**, which it was not before.

Found 2026-10-10 in the ISO by-number listing (142 holdings, read in full while
checking for irrigation standards). Emerson holds all three parts as separate
eDocs records, plus every technical circular:

| Document number | Part |
|---|---|
| `NACE_MR0175_ISO_15156-1` | Part 1: general principles for selection of cracking-resistant materials |
| `NACE_MR0175_ISO_15156-2` | Part 2: cracking-resistant carbon and low-alloy steels, and cast irons |
| `NACE_MR0175_ISO_15156-3` | Part 3: cracking-resistant CRAs and other alloys |
| `NACE_MR0175_ISO_15156_ALL` | Parts 1-3 bound together, older 2009 edition |
| `..._TC1` / `_TC2` / `_TC3` / `_TC4` | technical circulars against several editions |

The three current parts are the **2021/2020** edition, loaded 2022-11-07. That
settles the `edition: unverified` this entry carried for a day, without opening
the document: the listing states it.

**Part 3 is the one the catalogue will want** -- it governs the corrosion-resistant
alloys, which is where a stainless part's sour-service limit comes from.
