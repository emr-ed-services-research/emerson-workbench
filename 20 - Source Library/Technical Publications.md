# Technical Publications

Fisher / Emerson instruction manuals held in the Source Library, in
`20 - Source Library/Technical Publications/`. These are the current
authoritative maintenance references for the specific hardware taught in the
[[1400 — Course Home|1400]] course.

This is a locate-the-right-source catalogue: what each manual is and its scope
at a section level. Concept-level cross-referencing between manuals is
deliberately deferred until we have run a real task against the library.

Each manual follows the same house structure — Introduction / Scope,
Description, Specifications, Educational Services, Installation, Maintenance,
Parts Ordering, Parts List — so the scope notes below highlight what is
distinctive rather than repeat that skeleton.

## Sliding-stem actuators

### Fisher 667 Diaphragm Actuator
`d100310x012-667-diaphragm-actuator-sizes-30-30i-76-76i-and-87-fisher.pdf` ·
D100310X012 · May 2018

Spring-and-diaphragm sliding-stem actuator, sizes 30/30i–76/76i and 87 (the
667‑4 in sizes 70/70i and 87 is also covered). The 667 and 657 are the
complementary direct- / reverse-acting pair in Fisher's spring-and-diaphragm
line. **Installation:** mounting on the valve, discussion of bench
set, spring verification, stem connector assembly, friction discussion, deadband
measurement, loading connection. **Maintenance:** actuator, top-mounted
handwheel (adjustable down travel stop), side-mounted handwheels for the two
size groups, casing-mounted travel stops. Parts kits and full parts list.
Mounts on easy-e globe valves.

### Fisher 657 Diaphragm Actuator
`instruction-manual-fisher-657-diaphragm-actuator-sizes-30-30i-through-70-70i-87-en-11746662.pdf` ·
D100306X012 · June 2018

Spring-and-diaphragm sliding-stem actuator, sizes 30/30i–70/70i and 87 (plus the
657‑4). The companion to the 667 (opposite fail action). Same section coverage:
installation (mounting, bench set, spring verification, stem connector, friction,
deadband, loading connection); maintenance (actuator, top- and side-mounted
handwheels, casing-mounted adjustable up and down travel stops); parts.

### Fisher 585C Series Piston Actuators
`d102087x012.pdf` · D102087X012 · November 2022

Sliding-stem pneumatic piston actuator, sizes 25–130, in handwheel or
spring-return constructions. **Principle of operation** is given separately for
handwheel and spring-return builds. **Installation:** bypass assembly, three-way
valve application note, size 25/50 mounting, size 60–130 mounting.
**Maintenance** is split by size group (25/50 vs 60–130): replacing seals,
changing action, changing bias springs, handwheel-construction disassembly and
reassembly. The long-stroke 585CLS is a separate manual (D103793X012, not held).

## Rotary actuators

### Fisher 1051 and 1052 Style H & J Rotary Actuators
`d100320x012.pdf` · D100320X012 · July 2023

Spring-return diaphragm rotary actuator — 1051 in sizes 40 and 60, 1052 in sizes
40, 60 and 70 — with H and J mounting adaptations (H allows use on non-Fisher
equipment). **Adjustment:** 1052 spring adjustment, initial compression,
stroking range, turnbuckle adjustment. **Maintenance:** disassembly, assembly.
**Changing actuator mounting:** changing styles, changing positions.
Top-mounted handwheels and adjustable up/down travel stops.

### Fisher 1061 Pneumatic Piston Rotary Actuator (Style F & G)
`d100324x012.pdf` · D100324X012 · June 2017

Double-acting pneumatic piston rotary actuator, sizes 30–130, for splined valve
shafts. **Style G** mounting bracket is for Fisher 9500 valves only; **Style F**
is for all other rotary valves. Coverage: actuator mounting and changing
mounting, pressure connections, adjustment, disassembly and assembly, the
locking mechanism (installing and operating it), and the pipe-away vent. Parts
kits, actuator repair kits, pipe-away-vent retrofit kits, parts list.

### Fisher 2052 Diaphragm Rotary Actuator
`d103296x012.pdf` · D103296X012 · February 2023

Spring-and-diaphragm rotary actuator for throttling (with a positioner) or
on/off service (without), with an ISO 5211 mating interface for non-Fisher
valves. **Maintenance:** replacing the diaphragm; replacing the diaphragm plate,
diaphragm rod assembly and spring(s); changing or replacing the actuator lever.
**Positioner mounting** for the 3610, DVC6020 and DVC6200. Top-mounted
handwheel, locking mechanism, parts.

## Sliding-stem packing systems

The easy-e instruction manuals (ES / ED / ET / EZ) hand off to these two
dedicated manuals for the two live-loaded packing systems — adjustment is by
spring geometry, not flange torque, and the procedure lives only in these docs.
Both are used by [[1400 — Course Home|1400]] Chapter 2 Module 6 (Packing
Replacement & Adjustment). HIGH-SEAL is the higher pressure/temperature version
of ENVIRO-SEAL; the two are set by *different* methods.

### Fisher ENVIRO-SEAL Packing System for Sliding-Stem Valves
`d101642x012.pdf` · D101642X012 · July 2017 · 12 pp

Belleville (coned-disk) spring-loaded stem packing for 3/8"–2" stems, in
**double PTFE**, **single graphite ULF**, or **duplex** arrangements (7 springs
for PTFE/duplex, 9 for graphite ULF). **Tightening Procedures:** anti-seize the
studs and nuts (threads *and* nut faces), hand-tighten, then tighten alternately
and evenly — flange kept parallel — until the Belleville springs are 100% flat
(a sharp jump in nut torque). Then back each nut off to the 85% "target load":
**1/2 turn for PTFE or duplex, 1/4 turn for graphite ULF**. For graphite ULF,
Table 7 torque values are an alternative (no back-off). Also carries the
**20/20 rule** for judging a used packing bore (usable if < 20% pitted and no
pit deeper than 0.020 in) and the brake-hone cleaning method.

### Fisher HIGH-SEAL ULF Live-Loaded Packing System
`53688016.pdf` · D101453X012 · July 2017 · 12 pp

The higher pressure/temperature companion to ENVIRO-SEAL — a long-travel
Belleville spring pack with a **load scale** (zero / minimum / maximum spring
load lines) matched to the stem size. **Installation:** anti-seize and
hand-tighten the nuts; loosen the load-scale mounting screws and align the
bottom edge of the scale with the indicator disk while the springs are
uncompressed; then tighten the nuts alternately and evenly — flange parallel —
until the indicator disk reaches the **maximum-compression line**. No
retightening for the life of the packing unless the indicator drifts toward the
minimum line. (Filename `53688016` is the Emerson media number; document is
D101453X012.)

## Rotary valve bodies

### Fisher 9500 Butterfly Control Valve
`d100380x012.pdf` · D100380X012 · February 2019

Fully lined (nitrile or PTFE) wafer-style butterfly valve, NPS 2–12, with
conventional or FISHTAIL disk; Class VI shutoff. Sections: description;
installation; **adjustments** — hub seals, linkage, three-way valve tandem
linkage; **maintenance** — disassembly, reassembly, actuator mounting, changing
disk rotation and action; parts.

### Fisher 8580 Rotary Valve
`d103300x012.pdf` · D103300X012 · April 2020

High-performance double-offset (double-eccentric) butterfly valve, DN50–DN300 /
NPS 2–12, with interchangeable soft or metal seal; PN10–PN40, CL150 and CL300.
**Maintenance:** packing maintenance, replacing the seal ring assembly,
replacing the disk / shafts / bearings, actuator mounting. Parts.

### Fisher Vee-Ball V150, V200 and V300 Rotary Control Valves
`d101554x012.pdf` · D101554X012 · August 2023

Segmented V-notch ball valves, NPS 1–12 (Series B and non-Series-B).
**Maintenance:** packing maintenance; replacing the ball seal (disassembly,
assembly); HD ball seal lubrication; bearing and ball maintenance; welded taper
key replacement; rotary attenuator maintenance; Whisper NVX trim maintenance;
Cavitrol Hex installation. **Actuator mounting:** determining mounting position
and closed position. Parts; Appendix A for non-Series-B valves. ENVIRO-SEAL
rotary packing is covered in a separate manual (D101643X012, not held).

### Fisher V500 Rotary Globe (Eccentric Plug) Control Valve
`d100423x012.pdf` · D100423X012 · June 2026

Eccentric-plug rotary control valve, NPS 1–12, with metal or ceramic trim.
**Maintenance:** packing maintenance; replacing the retainer, seat ring and face
seals; replacing the valve plug, shaft and bearings; adjusting actuator travel;
changing valve flow direction; changing actuator mounting style. Parts; Appendix
A on flangeless considerations.
