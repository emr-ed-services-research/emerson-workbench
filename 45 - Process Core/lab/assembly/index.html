FV-101 — the whole control valve

A Fisher 657 size 45 on an ED NPS 4, with the fluid solved underneath it. The two were checked against their published mounting numbers before either was drawn — travel, stem diameter and yoke boss — so this is one machine rather than two pictures stacked up.

The actuator's origin is the bottom face of its yoke; the valve's is the top of its bonnet. Mounting one on the other is a single translation, which is the whole reason each layer was given an origin somebody chose on purpose. The spring extends as the valve opens — air above the diaphragm is what closes it.
