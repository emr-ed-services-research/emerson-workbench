1 · schem-view default theme — what is connected to what

Symbols and plain lines. No bore, no length, no scale: a process display answers “what is the loop”, and this answer is as true of a refinery as of a test bench.

2 · phys-view the line as pipe — but not to scale

Walls, bores and real elbow sweeps — but the bore is a fixed number of pixels, the same for any part, so what it works out to in inches depends entirely on the frame’s px-per-inch. Panel 4 measures what it comes to for this rig. Either way it is a render constant, not a dimension, which is why a sourced length had nowhere to land before the geometry layer existed.

3 · modern skin the stock chrome — there is no separate “pro” one

Flow colour, fluid motion, readouts and panel furniture, over the same schem-view geometry. A consumer overlays or replaces this; it does not fork the layers underneath it.

4 · geometry to scale — one scale for length and bore alike

NPS 10 Sch 40 as the catalogue has it, drawn at a single scale, with the schematic’s bore for the same line beneath it at that same scale. The ratio between them is measured, not asserted — and at this frame it is close to 1, which is a coincidence of px-per-inch rather than the schematic being to scale. This is the panel the other three cannot be.

5 · trim the cage’s own curve, from Catalog 12

Inherent against installed, with a linear reference behind them. The marker is where the slider is.
