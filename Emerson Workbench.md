# Emerson Workbench

A department-level operational LMS that houses all Emerson Educational Services
course content in one place. It replaces the current model of heavy, drift-prone
PowerPoint decks (100–300+ slides) paired with separate Word-based instructor and
student guides.

Content is authored in **Markdown** as the primary format and rendered out to the
artifacts instructors and students actually use.
