Reading the Inherent Characteristic Curve

  • Quick-opening
  • Linear
  • Equal-percentage

Quick-opening delivers most of its flow change early in travel, linear changes at a constant rate, and equal-percentage changes slowest near closed and fastest near full-open — read where a curve sits relative to the straight line to tell which characteristic a valve has.
