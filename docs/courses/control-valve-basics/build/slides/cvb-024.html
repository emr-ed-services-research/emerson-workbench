Bolted-Flange & Welded End Connections

  1. 1Flat-Face — matching flanges in full-face contact with the gasket; low-pressure cast iron/brass bodies
  2. 2Raised-Face — a circular raised sealing face; standard on Class 250 and steel/alloy bodies to 6000 psig
  3. 3Ring-Type Joint — a U-shaped groove with a metal ring gasket; to 15,000 psig on steel/alloy bodies
  4. 4Socket Weld-Ends — pipe slips into a bored socket, joined with a fillet weld; NPS 2 and smaller
  5. 5Butt Weld-Ends — beveled ends joined to the pipeline with a full-penetration weld; NPS 2-1/2 and larger

After Control Valve Handbook, 6th ed., Figs. 3.17, 3.18 — used directly, own printed labels retained; the two figures are combined into one image, no content altered. Component Index: cvh-cmp-bolted-flange-end-connections, cvh-cmp-welded-end-connections.
