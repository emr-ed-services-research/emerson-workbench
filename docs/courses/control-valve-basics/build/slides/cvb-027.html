How a Bellows Seal Bonnet Works

  1. 1ENVIRO-SEAL bellows seal bonnet — a fully-welded bellows path protects both the stem and the packing from the process fluid, for essentially zero stem leakage
  2. 2Welded-leaf bellows — thin metal diaphragms stacked into the flexible seal; shorter package height, service life may be limited
  3. 3Mechanically-formed bellows — hydroformed from tubing instead; taller, but a more repeatable process for higher reliability

After Control Valve Handbook, 6th ed., Figs. 3.22, 3.23, 3.24 (Mechanically-Formed Bellows, §3.3.2) — used directly, combined side by side. Component Index: cvh-cmp-enviroseal-bellows-seal-bonnet, cvh-cmp-welded-leaf-bellows, cvh-cmp-mechanically-formed-bellows.
