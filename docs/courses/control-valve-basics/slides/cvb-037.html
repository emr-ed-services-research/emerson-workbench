Double-Acting and Scotch-Yoke Piston Actuators

  1. 1Double-acting piston actuator — supply pressure on both sides of the piston gives maximum thrust in either direction, for high-force or fast-stroking service
  2. 2Scotch-yoke piston actuator — the same piston principle, converted to rotary valve-shaft motion through a yoke mechanism

After Control Valve Handbook, 6th ed., Figs. 3.46, 3.47 — used directly, combined side by side. Component Index: cvh-cmp-double-acting-piston-actuator, cvh-cmp-scotch-yoke-piston-actuator.
